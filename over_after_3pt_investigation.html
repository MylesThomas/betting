Phase 1 — Foundational (do first, stop if any fail)

Phase 1 is the kill switch. If 1.1 (real odds ROI) fails, the investigation ends — the edge is tax-absorbed. Do not proceed to Phase 2 without clearing all three checks here.
1.1
Actual OVER ROI using real over_odds
Most likely single point of failure
PASS

The assumed +12% ROI uses -110. Books typically post live props with asymmetric juice and may have the OVER at -120 or -130 in these moments — especially since they just moved the line up aggressively. Breakeven at -120 is 54.5%; at -130 is 56.5%. The raw 58.5% still has margin, but it's tight. Verify with actual odds from the signal data.

Approach
For each of the 191 DK trigger signals, compute:
decimal_over = (over_odds/100 + 1) if over_odds > 0 else (100/abs(over_odds) + 1)
Then: profit_if_win = (decimal_over - 1) * 100, profit_if_loss = -100
Also compute hold per signal: (1/decimal_over) + (1/decimal_under) - 1

Key findings from decomposition

  • MC model edge is irrelevant: Pearson r = 0.006. The OVER edge is structural — take it on every clean trigger, don't filter by model edge.
  • Jump magnitude is U-shaped: +0.5–1.0 (71.4% OVER, +36% ROI) and +3.0+ (80% OVER, +48% ROI) are the profitable zones. The +1.0–3.0 range is dead money (48–55%, roughly breakeven). Consider filtering to these extremes.
  • Hot hand is real: 2nd 3PT in a game hits OVER at 63.0% vs 54.4% for the 1st. Filter to 2nd+ or use as a weight multiplier.
  • Q4 is the best quarter: 67.7% OVER (n=31, CI: 53–80%). Q3 is largest group (n=91, 58.2%). Q1/Q2 are near breakeven on OVER ROI.
  • No market adaptation detected through May 2026.
