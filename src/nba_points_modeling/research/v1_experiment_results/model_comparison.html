NBA Points Props — v1 Model Comparison

6 approaches backtested  ·  Train: 2024-25  ·  Test: 2025-26 (out-of-sample)  ·  May 2026

TL;DR

No approach was profitable at -110 flat betting — but two clear signals are worth building on in v2.
  • Market Blending won on every metric: best Brier (0.24967), best ROI (−2.5%), highest win rate (51.1%). The key insight: isotonic recalibration found the market overprices low-probability overs by ~3.5%. That's the most actionable edge from this entire run.
  • Injury/lineup detection found a real signal — a 4.1pp over-rate lift when core players are absent — but the detector is broken: 98.5% of games got flagged, diluting everything. Fix the threshold and this becomes usable.
  • Rolling form features actively hurt (Agent 4 was 2nd worst). Players who recently beat their line are slightly more likely to fall under next game — regression to mean. Don't use streak features in v2.
  • XGBoost underperformed despite the most features. All features had ~equal importance (~9% each), meaning the model found no real hierarchy. The features themselves aren't wrong — the model just needs better signal.
  • Bayesian player model was the worst ROI (−10.1%). 351 of 377 players had positive bias, which is suspicious — almost certainly a data artifact from star players getting propped at conservative lines.

Cross-Model Findings

No approach was profitable at −110 flat betting
Break-even requires 52.38% win rate. Best model hit 51.1%. This is expected for a v1 baseline — NBA player props are a mature, competitive market. The question is whether there are narrow conditions where edge exists, not whether a naive model wins overall.
Market has a real low-probability over bias (+3.5pp)
When the no-vig over probability is in the bottom decile, actual hit rates are 3.5pp higher than market implied. This is the strongest signal found. It likely reflects bookmakers applying conservative vig on "prop specials" — low-probability outcomes where they model less accurately.
Absence signal is real but detector is broken
Agent 5 found a 4.1pp over-rate lift (44.3% → 48.4%) when a core rotation player is absent. However, 98.5% of games were flagged — the 35% appearance threshold is too loose. The signal exists in the feature importance (13.5%) despite the noise. Fix: raise threshold to >60%, require player averaged >28 min/game.
Rolling form / streak features actively hurt performance
Agent 4 (Recent Form) had the 2nd worst ROI. The pvl_r5 coefficient is negative — recent over-performance predicts under-performance next game. The market adjusts lines quickly for hot streaks. Chasing momentum is anti-edge here.
Star player lines are overpriced by −2.2pp
Agent 6 found that high-line players (stars) have a −2.2pp bias — meaning the market slightly overprices their over. This is consistent with "star premium" pricing where books know casual bettors prefer overs on famous players and shade the line accordingly.
Isotonic recalibration outperforms raw market and Platt scaling
The optimizer assigned 81% weight to isotonic regression and 18% to Platt scaling, discarding raw market entirely. This suggests the market probability ordering is good (Brier ranking) but the calibration is systematically off at the tails — exactly what isotonic regression corrects.

Recommended Next Steps for v2

  1. Validate the low-prob over bias directly (1-2 hours)
    Before building anything, confirm the +3.5pp bias holds cross-season. Filter to props where no-vig over probability <46%, check actual hit rate in 2023-24 and 2024-25 data. If it replicates, this is a standalone rule-based edge deployable today without a model.
  2. Fix the absence detector (threshold: >60% appearance, >28 min avg)
    Raise the core rotation threshold from 35% to 60% appearance rate AND require the player averaged >28 minutes per game. Rerun Agent 5's script and check what % of games get flagged — target 15-25%. If the 4.1pp lift holds with a sharper detector, this becomes the most valuable feature in v2.
  3. Build v2 as: isotonic calibration base + sharpened absence adjustment
    Use Agent 6's isotonic recalibration as the probability foundation, then add the fixed absence signal as a multiplicative adjustment. No rolling form features. Betting rule: edge ≥3% (raise from 2% to reduce noise) on games where low-prob over OR confirmed absence detected.
  4. Investigate the star-player under bias as a separate strategy
    Agent 6 found −2.2pp bias for high-line players. Backtest a simple rule: bet under on players with lines >25 pts when the market over probability is above the median. This is complementary to the low-prob over strategy and targets a different segment of the market.
