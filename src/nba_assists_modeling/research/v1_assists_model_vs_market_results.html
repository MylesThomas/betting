TL;DR
OLS vs market?
Beats market in both OOS folds — RMSE ~1% lower. Barely, but consistent.
XGB vs market?
Worse than market in both folds. Overfits on thin training data — 5K rows in fold 1 isn't enough.
Ridge vs market?
Beats market in both folds — nearly identical to OLS. Simple linear is the ceiling here.
Best single predictor?
max_line — RMSE 2.072 vs market 2.069. The posted line IS the signal. Rolling AST averages all lose to the market alone.
Is there a tradeable edge?
No — not yet. 1% RMSE improvement evaporates after vig. Need features the market hasn't priced: lineup changes, PG out at tip-off, pace/TO on the night.

Key finding — no feature beats the market alone. max_line comes closest (+0.003 RMSE) because the posted line is basically a proxy for the player's true expected assists. Why models beat market when combined: Even though no single feature beats market alone, OLS/Ridge finds that combining line features + rolling means gives a marginal correction — mainly in the low-line tier where the market's mechanical formula has more slack.

5. Decision & Next Steps

Gate result: FAIL for a tradeable model. OLS/Ridge beat market by ~1% RMSE — not enough to overcome vig at posted odds. XGB actively hurts. The current feature set is essentially a noisy re-derivation of what the bookmaker already computed.

What to try next (in priority order)

  • Lineup context — is the team's primary PG playing? When PG1 sits, backup PG's line is set before the news. This is the strongest hypothesis in the plan and can't be derived from rolling stats alone.
  • Opponent context — opponent defensive rating vs PG, steal rate (limits assist opportunities), team pace.
  • Home/away + days rest — small effect but free signal.
  • Low-tier focus (0.5–1.5 lines only) — the only tier where Ridge shows meaningful improvement (−2.6% RMSE). Retrain a focused model on this tier; n=4,008 OOS rows is sufficient.
  • More XGB data — fold 1 had only 5K rows. With 3 full seasons of data (32K rows) XGB may start adding value over linear.
