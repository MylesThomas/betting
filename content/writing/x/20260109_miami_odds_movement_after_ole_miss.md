# Miami Championship Odds Crater After Ole Miss Win - January 9, 2026

## CONTEXT & NOTES

**What Happened:**
- Miami beat Ole Miss 27-24 in CFP semifinals (Jan 9, 2026)
- Carson Beck's late TD covered Georgia -3 against Miami in earlier round
- Miami's championship futures moved dramatically in real-time after the win

**Odds Movement (Fanduel):**
- **Before Miami win**: +350 (fair: +344) with -0.3% vig = rare bettor advantage
- **After Miami win**: +190 (fair: +190) with +0.1% vig
- **Price change**: 84% shorter in minutes (160 points on american odds)
- **Vig shift**: From bettor advantage (-0.3%) to house edge (+0.1%)

**Ole Miss Movement:**
- **Before**: +585 avg (fair: +636) with +1.0% vig
- **After**: +636 (eliminated)

**Market Context:**
- Miami was the ONLY negative vig spot in the entire futures market before the game
- That edge evaporated immediately after the result
- Books adjusted instantly to remove the bettor advantage
- Fair odds jumped from +344 to +190 after eliminating Ole Miss

**Key Insight:**
- The negative vig on Miami before the game suggests books knew something
- Public was likely hammering Ole Miss or other teams
- Sharp action or book positioning created temporary bettor advantage
- Market corrected immediately once uncertainty was removed
