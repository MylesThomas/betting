### Key Value Spot: GREEN BAY PACKERS
- Record: 9-7-1 (barely .500, 56.25% win rate)
- Average market odds: +2500 (4.62% implied)
- Best available: +2500 @ DraftKings
- **Fair odds: +2381 (4.03% fair prob)**

- This creates negative vig opportunity (~5% relative value)

### Why Packers Have Value:
1. Below .500 record (9-7-1) means public isn't betting them
2. Books need to balance liability on chalk (Seahawks, Rams, Broncos)
3. Market punishing them for mediocre regular season
4. But fair odds show they're underpriced relative to their actual Super Bowl chances

### Market Context:
- Average vig across all books: 14.6%
- Top favorites (chalk tax):
  - Seahawks: +425 avg (fair: +450) = 14-3 record, 82% win rate
  - Rams: +450 avg (fair: +514) = 12-5 record, 71% win rate
  - Broncos: +700 avg (fair: +783) = 14-3 record, 82% win rate
- All chalk is overpriced with positive vig

### Other Negative Vig Opportunities (For Comparison):
- 49ers: +3000 avg (fair: +2865) = 12-5 record, slight value
- Chargers: +3300 avg (fair: +3306) = 11-6 record, near-fair pricing

**The Story**: Market is pricing based on regular season records, not Super Bowl probability. Packers' mediocre record makes them unpopular, creating value. Books overprice chalk to balance their books, giving up edge on teams nobody's betting.
