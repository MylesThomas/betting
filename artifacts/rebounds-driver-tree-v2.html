From yhat to edge — the pricing chain

yhat_adj = consensus_line + (1 − shrink) × (yhat − consensus_line)
σ = per-player rolling residual std (or global fallback)
P(under) = norm.cdf(line, loc=yhat_adj, scale=σ)
P_book = implied_prob(under_odds) ← raw vigged, NOT de-vigged
edge = P(under) − P_book ← bet when edge > min_edge
σ drives the confidence interval around yhat — wider σ means flatter P(under) curves and smaller edges at the same line distance. Per-player rolling σ (roll_reb_std_5) outperforms a global constant because variance is player-specific (bigs are more consistent than wings).

Forward-looking dynamics

Not part of the static structure. Apply as overlays when evaluating model durability and signal decay.

Outcome / volume drivers

Pace inflation (league-wide)
Average possessions/game has risen ~3% over 5 years; more shot attempts = more total rebounds, but individual lines adjust slowly
Data source: NBA API team pace stats / TBD
3PA rate structural shift
League 3PA rate has risen from ~30% to ~40% of FGA over a decade; 3s miss at ~66% and rebound further — inflates total rebounds and reduces individual REB predictability
Data source: NBA API shot chart data / TBD
Stretch-big role expansion
Traditional rebounding bigs replaced by shooting-first PFs; concentrated REB opportunity moving toward a smaller pool of true bigs
Data source: positional usage trend data / TBD

Model accuracy drivers

Role change lag (60g window)
60-game rolling mean is slow to incorporate trade deadline moves, coaching changes, or injury-return role adjustments — consider 10-20g window as a blend
Data source: rebounds_feature_spec.py / S3
Missing features (data gaps)
Opponent frontcourt reb rate, lineup REB competition, and projected minutes are in the outcomes tree but absent from the current feature spec — largest gap between DGP and model
Data source: rebounds_feature_spec.py (current gaps)
σ calibration drift
Per-player rolling σ is well-specified but may understate variance for returning injured players whose 5g window is thin
Data source: edge_calibration.py / model residuals

Market / line dynamics

Market efficiency creep
Books have tightened rebounds lines as sharp volume has grown; the spread_signed and 3PA signals were likely larger edges in 2022-23 than they are today
Data source: line movement analysis (TBD)
Shrinkage optimum shifts
Optimal shrinkage increases as market efficiency improves — re-sweep shrink parameter each season rather than locking in a prior-year value
Data source: edge_calibration.py sweep results
Line anchor quality
Consensus line quality varies by book depth; early-morning lines have more disagreement (wider min/max spread) and thus more potential edge before sharpening
Data source: The Odds API (bookmaker list)
Reading the trees. Tree 1 (green): Player REB = Minutes × Per-Minute REB Rate. Minutes splits into Role/Usage and Game Script Risk. Per-Minute Rate splits into Individual Tendency and Game Context. Tree 2 (blue): yhat = Baseline + Adjustments. Baseline anchors to historical tendency and the consensus line. Adjustments move the prediction based on game-specific context and shrinkage. Pricing chain: yhat + σ + line → P(under) via parametric normal → edge = P(under) − P_book. The gap between Tree 1 leaves and Tree 2 leaves is the model's missing-feature backlog.
