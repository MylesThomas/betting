Forward-looking trends

Not part of the static structure. MECE within each column. Apply as overlays when evaluating strategy durability.

Volume drivers

Schedule compression (playoffs)
Play-in and playoff slates shrink to 2-4 games/night, halving available props
Data source: NBA schedule / nba-season-structure wiki
Prop market expansion
Additional sportsbooks entering state markets increases props per player and boosts line depth
Data source: The Odds API bookmaker list (TBD)
Threshold calibration drift
Optimal min-edge cutoff shifts as season-long sample grows; looser threshold = more bets at lower ROI
Data source: edge_calibration.py sweep results

Edge / ROI drivers

Model staleness
OLS/XGB trained on prior seasons; mid-season role changes (trade deadline, injury returns) degrade feature relevance until retrain
Data source: prod_train_rebounds_models.py retraining log
Rolling window lag
60-game rolling mean is slow to capture sudden minutes/role changes; shorter windows (10-20g) may track better but add noise
Data source: rebounds_feature_spec.py / S3 feature universe
Market efficiency creep
Books are tightening rebounds lines seasonally as sharp action identifies the same signals; edges compress over multi-year horizon
Data source: TBD (line movement analysis)

Mix shift

Pace-driven rebound inflation
Higher-pace teams generate more missed shots and thus total rebounds; individual lines may lag team-level pace shifts
Data source: NBA API team pace stats (TBD)
Defensive scheme mix
Zone defense increases long-range miss rate and unpredictable rebound locations; reduces individual REB predictability
Data source: expert interviews / TBD
Player role concentration
Seasonal drift toward fewer "designated rebounder" roles (stretch bigs replacing traditional PFs) shifts REB distribution toward star players
Data source: NBA position trend data (TBD)
