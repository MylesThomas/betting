### Overtime Handling

- Props INCLUDE overtime unless specified
- Some props explicitly say "regulation only"
- Always check terms
- Historical data: ensure OT included/excluded consistently

### Injuries Mid-Game

- Player gets injured early in game
- Props usually stand (not voided)
- Rare exceptions: book may void if player doesn't play significant time
- Check book rules

### Trade Deadline

- Player traded mid-season
- Update player-team mappings immediately
- Historical game logs need careful handling
- Props may be unavailable during transition

### Load Management / DNP-Rest

- Star players sitting out
- Affects opponent props (easier matchup?)
- Affects teammate props (more usage?)
- Check injury reports before betting

### Back-to-Back Games

- NBA teams play consecutive nights
- Fatigue affects performance
- Starters may have reduced minutes
- Historical: filter for rest days vs back-to-backs

### Blowouts

- Garbage time affects props
- Starters sit in 4th quarter
- Bench players get more minutes
- Consider game script when analyzing
