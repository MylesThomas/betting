## Executive Summary

After analyzing **all** NBA points props strategies across 3 seasons, we identified clear patterns in what works and what doesn't:

### 🎯 Key Findings

1. **UNDER strategies dominate**: 9 of 9 profitable strategies are UNDERs
2. **OVER strategies fail**: 0 of 11 OVER strategies are profitable
3. **Only 1 strategy profitable all 3 seasons**: `20_25_2_6_fav_under` (2D)
4. **2D strategies more reliable**: 50% profitable vs 25% for 3D
5. **Best overall edge**: 15.9% ROI (`20_25_2_6_dog_rim_under`)
