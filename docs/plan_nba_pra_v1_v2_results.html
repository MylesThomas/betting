PRA Research Results — v1 & v2

2026-05-25  |  Market: player_points_rebounds_assists  |  32,305 player-games · 3 seasons  |  Spread coverage fixed to 99.8%

TL;DR

Flat rule: Betting every PRA under loses money in all three seasons (–7.5% ROI overall). No tier passes the gate. The market prices PRA efficiently at the aggregate level.

Model vs market: The prop line (median) is already the best single predictor of PRA (MAE 6.34, R² 0.50). Every rolling feature alone is individually worse than the line. OLS and Ridge on top of the line improve by ~0.01 MAE consistently across both folds. XGBoost can't beat the market — it overfits on rolling features.

min/max line as inputs: Dominate XGBoost importance (56% + 23%) but essentially just reconstruct the consensus median. Net improvement over using the median directly is negligible (~0.01 MAE).

Spread (fixed): Spread join was broken at 33% coverage due to a UTC vs Eastern date mismatch. Fixed by converting game_time to Eastern time before extracting date. Now 99.8% coverage. Spread adds almost no signal — only 1.25% XGBoost importance. Adding it doesn't change OLS/Ridge results meaningfully.

Edge calibration (new signal): In the 2025-26 out-of-sample fold, XGBoost edges ≥ 2.0 points hit at 57% with +6.6% ROI (n=1,257). At ≥ 3.0 points: +7.9% ROI (n=349). This does NOT replicate in 2024-25 (negative at all thresholds). Inconsistent — don't bet yet.

One structural signal: The oracle minutes test (same-game MIN 15–25) shows +15–67% ROI across all line tiers. Not exploitable yet — needs a pre-game minutes proxy to use it.

Spread Join: Root Cause & Fix

Fixed. Coverage: 33% → 99.8%.

Root cause: Game lines store game_time as UTC (2024-10-22 23:40:00+00:00). The original query extracted the date from the UTC timestamp via regex. NBA games starting at 8pm+ ET are past midnight UTC, so they extract as next day in UTC but the NBA API logs them as the ET calendar date. Example: Lakers at 10pm ET Oct 22 = 2am UTC Oct 23 → spread had game_date Oct 23, logs had Oct 22 → no join.

Fix applied: Replaced UTC date extraction with Eastern time conversion in the DuckDB query:

CAST(CAST(game_time AS TIMESTAMPTZ) AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York' AS DATE) AS game_date

Also fixed: "Los Angeles Clippers" → "LA Clippers" team name normalisation (odds API vs NBA API).

Remaining unmatched (0.3%): 8 (game_date, team) pairs across 4 specific dates — likely cancelled/postponed games not captured by the odds API.
