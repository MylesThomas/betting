Key Findings

1. Deduping consistently improves ROI
At every edge threshold in both IS and OOS, the deduped version has higher ROI than raw. The raw strategy isn't finding more independent edges — it's just betting the same outcome 7–8 times through different books. True edge per opportunity is better than the raw results suggest.
2. The 2–4 book bucket is a red flag (IS)
In IS, bets where 2–4 books offer the line have 37.3% win rate and −17.9% ROI at the canonical strategy — far below the break-even implied by the odds. The 5+ book bucket drives all the returns (51.4% win rate, +11.6% ROI). OOS sample is too small to confirm (54 bets), but the IS signal warrants filtering to 5+ books only before relying on the 2–4 bucket.
3. Live Lambda implication
The Lambda is currently placing per-book bets. On a typical 8-book qualifying slot, that's 8 correlated bets vs. 1 if deduped. A bad player day hits you 8× harder than the backtest ROI implies. Consider switching to best-book-only bets in the Lambda — this halves apparent position volatility and aligns live sizing with the deduped backtest.
4. Canonical strategy (edge≥5, dogs, 1.5) still holds OOS
Deduped OOS: +14.8% ROI (n=120). Raw OOS: +7.5% (n=443). Both are positive and consistent with IS. The edge appears real; the raw n overstates independence.
