Key findings

  • Lines 2.5–6.5 are well calibrated — calibration gaps <2pp in either direction. Market is efficient for role players and bench depth.
  • Lines 7.5–10.5 show moderate under edge (+2.3pp to +4.8pp) — market slightly underprices the under at mid-tier big lines.

Step 4 — Calibration Params (Option A)

Method:
Option A Normal CDF — closed-form, no separate calibration model.

Formula:
mean_adj = consensus + (1 − shrink) × (yhat − consensus)
sigma = max(roll_reb_std_5, sigma_floor)
P(under) = Φ((line − mean_adj) / sigma)
edge_under = P(under) − P(under)_book_vigged

Calibration parameters

ParamValueRationale
shrinkage0.0No shrinkage — mean_adj = yhat. Swept [0.0, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75] in v3; 0.0 produced best out-of-sample P&L. Full model signal is used without pulling toward consensus.
sigma_colroll_reb_std_5Rolling 5-game std dev. Swept windows [5, 10, 20] in v3; window=5 selected.
sigma_floor0.25Prevents division by zero for players with zero variance over last 5 games (e.g., debut player, consistent 0-rebound stints).
edge basisvs raw vigged implied probEdge computed against raw vigged (not novig) implied probability. Means edge_under slightly understates true edge. Conservative choice.
