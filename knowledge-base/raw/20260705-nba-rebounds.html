Market type:
Integer stat, variable line market. REB is always an integer; lines are half-values (3.5, 4.5, 5.5, etc.). No pushes possible. Per-book lines vary (typically ±0.5). Most similar to NFL rec yards in structure.

Strategic prior:
Rebounds are rotation- and matchup-sensitive. Books lag real-world changes in role (injury replacements, lineup shifts, pace changes). Rotation-signal lag → exploitable edge on UNDER when a player's role has quietly shrunk but book line hasn't adjusted.

Key findings

  • Lines 2.5–6.5 are well calibrated — calibration gaps <2pp in either direction. Market is efficient for role players and bench depth.
  • Lines 7.5–10.5 show moderate under edge (+2.3pp to +4.8pp) — market slightly underprices the under at mid-tier big lines.
  • Lines 11.5+ show strong structural under mispricing — gaps grow monotonically: +5.3pp at 11.5, +8.8pp at 13.5, +14.9pp at 15.5. The market prices elite-big over bets as ~57.5% probable when they actually hit only 27.6% of the time at line 15.5.
  • This is the structural mispricing the strategy exploits. The UNDER-only strategy fires most aggressively at high lines (Jokic, Giannis, Wembanyama) where the book systematically overprices the over because recreational bettors root for stars.
  • Vig is flat at ~6.9% across all lines — no change in market certainty by line bucket.

Step 4 — Calibration Params (Option A)

Method:
Option A Normal CDF — closed-form, no separate calibration model.

Formula:
mean_adj = consensus + (1 − shrink) × (yhat − consensus)
sigma = max(roll_reb_std_5, sigma_floor)
P(under) = Φ((line − mean_adj) / sigma)
edge_under = P(under) − P(under)_book_vigged

Calibration parameters

ParamValueRationale
shrinkage0.0No shrinkage — mean_adj = yhat. Swept [0.0, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75] in v3; 0.0 produced best out-of-sample P&L. Full model signal is used without pulling toward consensus.
sigma_colroll_reb_std_5Rolling 5-game std dev. Swept windows [5, 10, 20] in v3; window=5 selected.
sigma_floor0.25Prevents division by zero for players with zero variance over last 5 games (e.g., debut player, consistent 0-rebound stints).
edge basisvs raw vigged implied probEdge computed against raw vigged (not novig) implied probability. Means edge_under slightly understates true edge. Conservative choice.

Step 6 — In-Sample Grid Search

In-sample testing was not run separately for rebounds because the production pipeline retrains daily on all available data before scoring. There is no fixed "version-pinned" model to evaluate in-sample. The out-of-sample leave-one-season-out results in Step 5 are the appropriate performance benchmark.

The in-sample / out-of-sample ratio concept applies to fixed-model pipelines (e.g. NBA points model, MLB strikeouts) where a model trained once is then evaluated on held-out data. For rebounds, since the model rebuilds each gameday, the relevant check is whether out-of-sample strategy metrics are stable across seasons — which they are (3.61% OLS mean ROI, 2 of 3 seasons positive).
