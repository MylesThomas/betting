Consistent with other MLB markets (total bases, pitcher strikeouts) where recreational bettors over-bet overs.

Hypothesis entering modeling: UNDER bets at 1.5 line likely hold the most exploitable edge.

Key Findings

  • Line = 0.5: LR direct (Brier=0.2384) barely beats market (0.2385). Poisson CDF is worse (0.2401, max gap 0.063).
  • Line = 1.5: Market is slightly best (0.2231). LR and Poisson CDF both slightly worse (0.2240/0.2241).
  • The market is already nearly optimally calibrated at the aggregate level — LR offers minimal Brier improvement.
  • Edge will come from systematic miscalibration at specific odds buckets and directions (found in Step 1.5: UNDER edge of ~6pp at 1.5), not from overall model accuracy improvement.

Step 4 — Poisson CDF vs Logistic Calibration (REBUILD)

Goal: Convert Ridge OOF prediction (yhat = projected raw K count) into P(over line) / P(under line) using only book-invariant features. Compared two methods: Poisson CDF and Logistic calibration.

Method Definitions

MethodInputsMechanismAssumption
Poisson CDFyhat (Ridge OOF)K ~ Poisson(mu=yhat); P(over line) = 1 - Poisson.cdf(floor(line), mu)K distribution is Poisson (mean = variance)
Logistic calibrationyhat + offered_lineOOF LogisticRegression(yhat, line) → P(over); cv=5, temporal sortLearns actual K distribution from OOF data

Both methods are book-invariant: same yhat for every book row at the same (player, game_date, offered_line). The offered_line input to logistic is fine because it's not a market price — it's just the boundary value (0.5 or 1.5) needed to compute P(over/under).

Calibration by Line — Key Finding

Poisson has a 6.77pp systematic miscalibration at line 1.5. Batter Ks are underdispersed (variance ≈ 0.75 < mean ≈ 0.89). Poisson assumes mean=variance, which doesn't hold. It predicts P(over 1.5) = 28.08% when the actual rate is 34.85% — generating phantom edge_under signals.

Candidate Config Deep-Dive: consensus_line 1.5_only

What it's doing mechanically: At line=1.5, 99.5% of rows have consensus_yhat=1.5 (same line, same book). The logistic receives nearly constant (1.5, 1.5) input → nearly constant P(under) ≈ 65%. Edge = 65% − raw_implied_prob_under. So consensus_line 1.5_only is purely a price filter: "bet every 1.5 UNDER where the book is pricing the under below the historical 65% hit rate." No player skill signal at all. It's exploiting the market's systematic mis-pricing of the over/under at line 1.5.
