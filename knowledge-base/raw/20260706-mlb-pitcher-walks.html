Observations

  • Market features dominate (expected): novig_prob_over is the single best predictor (0.604). The raw implied prob min/max features (0.582–0.589) are essentially proxies for the same signal across books.
  • Odds bins show genuine discriminative signal: consensus_under_odds_bin at 0.585 is the strongest categorical. When books price the under as a favorite (i.e., the pitcher is expected to walk few batters), that pricing is meaningfully correlated with outcome. The granular 8-bucket version performs similarly at 0.536.
  • Rolling walks are weak: walks_roll_career (0.517) is the best rolling feature — worse than every market-derived feature. Walks per start have very high variance (σ≈1.4). The best combined model will likely use market implied prob as the primary predictor and rolling walks as a soft correction.
  • Line-based features (consensus_line, min_line, max_line) have real OOS R² for the continuous target (0.045–0.052) — they tell the model what "tier" of pitcher this is and thus the expected walk level. They're weak for binary AUC but useful as features in an OLS regression predicting raw walks.
  • Drop from combo model: opponent quality (0.471), team context (≤0.481), related pitcher stats (innings, pitches, strikeouts all ≤0.497) — all below random on OOS. Not adding signal.
  • games_into_season (0.543): Modest signal — pitchers may walk more early in season. Worth including in combo as a numeric feature despite degenerate classifier behavior.
