## The Bryce Young problem

Bryce Young 2025 career highs: 64% comp, 3,011 yds, 23 TDs, 11 INTs — but ranked among 36 qualifying QBs:
- **#32 EPA per attempt** (-0.05)
- **#30 success rate**
- **#35 yards per attempt** (6.3)
- Average air yards: **6.5** (#39 out of 45 QBs)
- Rate of passes thrown behind LOS: **20.1%** (#31)
- First-quarter air yards per attempt: **#634 of 643** QBs in 20-year database

The staff neutered his downfield passing to prevent errors. The result: they won games only because opponents were also bad. Against the 4th easiest pass defense schedule in the NFL, Young produced these numbers.

In 2026 they face the **3rd-hardest schedule**. Against actual competition, these numbers will not hold.

---

## The luck problem

Carolina's 8-9 record was built almost entirely on variance:
- **7-3 in 1-score games** (4th in NFL) — teams this good in close games regress
- **87.5% of wins came by single score** — 2nd-highest share in the league
- **3 of 8 wins** came from behind entering the 4th quarter (37.5% — 4x the rate for losing-record teams)
- Led at halftime in only 4 games; first-quarter scoring: **#26**

They weren't a good team that won. They were a bad team that maximized luck.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Coaching continuity helps Young; Year 3 with same staff
- Jaelan Phillips (18.8% pressure rate) could immediately fix the 31st-ranked pass rush
- Tetairoa McMillan in Year 2 with increased scheme trust
- Jalen Coker emerged as a legit weapon in final 7 games
- Carolina plays in the NFC South — worst division record vs. non-division opponents (.375) over last 4 years
- **Net rest advantage is real** — 5 rest-advantage games

**Under argument**
- Bryce Young's 2025 was career highs at #32 EPA/att — these are not good numbers under any framing
- Schedule goes from 4th-easiest to **3rd-hardest** in 2026 — brutal transition
- 87.5% of wins in 1-score games; historical regression rates are severe for these teams
- RB unit is #29 with Hubbard starting; run game was a weakness
- Receivers (#28) lack a true second option if McMillan is well-covered
- Market is pricing Panthers as ascending (odds improving every year) — classic trap

**Sharp lean**: Under. The schedule jump alone should cost 1-2 wins. The regression from 7-3 to ~.500 in close games alone would cost 2-3 more. Bryce Young's numbers were not winning numbers. Fade Carolina in win total futures.

## Prop angles

- **Tetairoa McMillan**: Target share over props in Year 2 (25.4% in Year 1 with restrictive coaching; if scheme opens up, he could lead the NFC in targets). Under on yardage totals early season while Young's effectiveness is uncertain.
- **Bryce Young**: Fade passing stat props vs. difficult defenses. His air yards ceiling is genuinely limited by scheme. In 2025 he completed only 42.7% of passes thrown 10+ yards downfield (#28) — do not bet deep ball production.
- **Jalen Coker**: Sleeper target share; he was the de facto WR2 down the stretch. Low ADP makes him valuable in prop markets if Legette underperforms.
- **Chuba Hubbard**: Under on volume vs. heavy boxes — Panthers O-line is #12 (decent), but #29 RBs ranking signals the unit won't generate chunk runs.
