# Los Angeles Chargers 2026

**Sharp forecast**: 10.5 wins — **Division Rank #2 (AFC West)**

---

## Coaching & scheme

- HC: **Jim Harbaugh** (Year 2; HC rank #12; two straight 11-win seasons; most conservative HC in NFL on 4th down — 4th-and-2 from own 40, goes for it just 47% — league's lowest)
- OC: **Mike McDaniel** (new; replaces Greg Roman; fired from Miami at 7-10; Shanahan coaching tree — outside zone, condensed formations, highest pre-snap motion rate in football, manufactured YAC)

## The Herbert–OL connection: the entire story

The Chargers went 11-6 in 2025 despite Herbert being hit **twice as many times as the QB with the most dropbacks in the NFL.** That this team won 11 games while Herbert took 54 sacks and 129 hits is the most remarkable finding in this chapter.

The splits tell the whole story:

| Condition | Comp% | AYPA | YPA | TD% | INT% | Sack% |
|-----------|-------|------|-----|-----|------|-------|
| With Alt (184 dropbacks) | **67.5%** | **9.1** | **8.3** | **7.1%** | 1.9% | 8.3% |
| Without Alt (431 dropbacks) | 65.9% | 7.3 | 6.9 | 4.2% | 2.8% | 10.1% |

With Alt: Herbert would have been the highest EPA-per-dropback QB in the NFL. Without Alt: a middling starter dragged down by the worst OL protection in the league.

For added context, **Rashawn Slater** signed a 4-year, $114M extension in July 2025 — and tore his patellar tendon 11 days later in routine training camp practice.

Both bookend tackles are 100% of the 2026 bet.

## Greg Roman's scheme sabotage

Roman dropped pre-snap motion from **#8 usage (2024) to #29 (2025)** and play action from **#3 usage to #21**. The data showing why this was destructive:

| Metric | With PSM | Without PSM |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| EPA/att | #8 | #29 |
| Success rate | #6 | #27 |
| Y/A | #6 | #22 |

| Metric | With PA | Without PA |
|--------|---------|------------|
| EPA/att | #12 | #28 |
| Success rate | #13 | #25 |
| Y/A | #9 | #16 |

"WHY IN THE WORLD would Roman drop motion to 48% (#29) after using it nearly 60% of the time in 2024 (#8) when it was producing those types of results?" — Sharp

**Mike McDaniel's Shanahan-tree system** runs motion at the highest rate in football and uses play action on ~44% of early-down passes. The system has made Garoppolo, Griese, Schaub, Tagovailoa, Carr, Cousins, and Darnold look above average. Herbert is a 6'6" two-threat passer who is everything Tagovailoa (the primary foil) is not.

## The 2025 record was inflated

The 11-win record rested on several factors that don't carry forward:
- **#8 easiest schedule** in 2025 (played #2 toughest in 2026 from Week 3 on)
- **Cameron Dicker FGA EPA: +20.46 (#7)** — excellent kicker, sustainable
- **6-2 in 1-score games (.750, #3)**; **4-1 in FG margin games (.800, #4)** — close-game luck regresses
- **Jesse Minter's defense allowed TDs on just 46.9% of RZ trips (#4)** — Minter left for Baltimore
- 55% of their 11 wins came by exactly one score

The 2026 schedule is the #2 toughest from Week 3 on with the worst rest structure in the league.

## Red zone: the persistent plague

Under Roman:
- Scored TDs on **47.4% of red zone trips (#29)**; 50.6% comp in RZ (#28); 5.2% INT rate in RZ
- Scored just **1 offensive TD across two consecutive postseason appearances**

Since 2022 same sample frame:
- Chargers RZ conversions: **#23 (53%)**
- Dolphins RZ conversions: **#7 (60%)** under McDaniel

McDaniel is a direct fix. Whether his system translates to Allegiant-level red zone performance within Year 1 is the key question.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Both starting tackles (Slater + Alt) returning healthy transforms the single biggest weakness — Herbert with a functional OL is a top-3 QB
- Mike McDaniel's scheme is structurally designed to maximize what Herbert does best (pre-snap motion, play action, outside zone); Roman was suppressing it
- Omarion Hampton in McDaniel's zone scheme could unlock the run game — Miami RBs ranked 5th in YPC (4.7), 5th in success, 1st in first-down rate under this system
- Tuli Tuipulotu's 17.9% pressure rate is the best among qualifying rushers in the NFL; with better supporting cast, the pass rush ascends
- 2026 schedule opens easy (Weeks 1-2 are ARI and LV) to let the OL chemistry build

**Under argument**
- **Net rest #32** — -24 days is the worst disadvantage since 2013; 7 rest-disadvantage games is a franchise record; plays 4 opponents off a bye
- 6-2 in 1-score games (.750) and 4-1 in FG margin games (.800) are both elite luck marks that regress; 55% of their wins came by 1 score
- Schedule jumps from #8 easiest (2025) to #2 toughest from Week 3 on
- Jesse Minter's defensive identity is gone — O'Leary inherits the scheme but not the play-caller who produced it
- WR room is **#19** with no proven elite target outside McConkey; only 14% of receptions gained 20+ yards (#25)
- Harbaugh is **#30 in 4th-down rate, #31 in 4th-down EPA** — the most conservative decision-maker in football leaves wins on the field in close games
- Herbert's playoff record: 0-3 (1 offensive TD in two postseason appearances under Roman)

**Sharp lean**: Neutral/slight Under. The improvements are real — Slater + Alt + McDaniel is a legitimate step-change. But the schedule and rest structure are genuinely the worst in modern Chargers history, close-game luck must regress, and the team still plays in the AFC West where they must beat the Chiefs and defend against the Ravens-level caliber opponents. 10.5 wins with this rest structure requires Herbert to finally validate the perception.

## Prop angles

- **Justin Herbert**: Wait for Week 3+ before buying any prop — the early schedule eases and rest advantages skew the early numbers; but full-season YPA over is live if both tackles play 17 games. Do NOT pay early-season prices that bake in a healthy OL until confirmed.
- **Omarion Hampton**: Rush attempts over in second half of season — McDaniel's system is run-heavy; Hampton's zone-scheme upside is real; but injury history (9 games as rookie) warrants fade on early-season volume.
- **Tuli Tuipulotu**: Sacks over — highest pressure rate in NFL for qualifying rushers; add Mesidor on the other side drawing attention; 15+ sack ceiling if healthy.
- **Ladd McConkey**: Receptions over — likely to become the first-read in McDaniel's slot/motion role; target efficiency history is elite.
- **Game totals**: Lean Under on Chargers road games vs top-10 defenses in Weeks 6-13 (rest disadvantage + schedule); Lean Over on Chargers home games in Weeks 1-5 (healthy OL + McDaniel learning curve).
