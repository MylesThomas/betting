## Jordan Love: elite talent behind a bad OL

Love's 2025 stats are severely distorted by a collapsing offensive line. Despite **38.6% pressure rate (#12 — among the highest in the NFL)**:

- **No pressure EPA: 0.56 (#1)** — best in the NFL when clean
- **Clean rating: 129.4 (Rk 1)** — historic clean performance
- **Man coverage EPA: 0.37 (#2)**
- **EPA/pass: 0.22 (Rk 2)** overall — top-3 despite the pressure
- **Layup throws: 0.31 (#1)** — maximizes value on routine throws

Under pressure: -0.37 (#21) — average under duress, but the sheer volume is the problem. With a functional OL, Love's ceiling is top-3 QB in the NFL. The OL at #27 is the most direct threat to his production.

---

## The weapons situation

2025 was a historic confluence of injuries: **Watson, Doubs, Reed, Kraft — all missed virtually the entire season**. Zero games with all four healthy simultaneously.

In 2026, all four are expected to return. This is a massive potential upgrade from a unit that was forced to play with depth-level receivers all season. **Receivers jump from #14 on paper but may be significantly underrated if all four are healthy.**

Josh Jacobs' availability is a wildcard. If he's suspended or unavailable, the RB unit (#16) gets significantly weaker.

---

## The Micah Parsons saga

Green Bay traded for Parsons at the price of:
- Kenny Clark (IDL)
- Two 1st-round picks
- $188M extension ($47M/yr)

Parsons went 12.5 sacks in 14 games before tearing his ACL. He is expected to start 2026 on the PUP list, potentially missing the first 4 weeks. When healthy, he transforms the Front 7. But the pass rush in early weeks is dangerously thin: Gary is gone, Enagbare is gone, Parsons is hurt.

---

## The OL collapse

The line fell from **#10 to #27** — one of the sharpest drops in the league:
- Jordan Morgan at LT has never started there in his career
- Love's pressure rate hit 38.6% (among highest in NFL) in 2025
- The line's collapse is the single biggest threat to GB's 2026 performance — if Morgan can't hold up, Love gets hit 40%+ of dropbacks

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Jordan Love is a top-3 QB talent (Rk 1 when clean, Rk 2 EPA/pass overall despite massive OL problems)
- Watson + Doubs + Reed + Kraft all returning from injury — receivers could be dramatically underrated
- Parsons transforms the defense when healthy (Week 5+)
- Gannon's scheme may unlock a different defensive identity than Barry's
- Division #2 path is favorable

**Under argument**
- OL dropped from #10 to #27 with Morgan untested at LT — Love takes a beating behind this line
- Parsons is on PUP list potentially through Week 4; early schedule could be 0-4 with no pass rush and a shaky line
- Josh Jacobs arrested (May 2026 felony domestic violence) — RB room has existential uncertainty
- **Games off road SNF/MNF: #1 worst** in the NFL; rest structure is the worst in football
- **Negated bye: #29** — another structural disadvantage
- 10.5 wins requires a fully healthy unit that missed 2025 entirely, a first-time LT, and an injured EDGE to return quickly

**Sharp lean**: Neutral. Love is elite. But the OL collapse, Parsons' PUP timeline, Jacobs' legal situation, and the worst rest structure in the NFL create significant downside risk. A healthy GB is a 12-win team; an injured GB is a 7-win team.

## Prop angles

- **Jordan Love**: TDs over, YPA over — when clean he is the #1 QB in the NFL; if OL improves even marginally, his stats will spike. Avoid early-season confidence props when Parsons/Gary aren't present.
- **Micah Parsons**: Sacks over in second half of season (post-PUP return); if active from Week 5+, a 10-14 sack pace is realistic.
- **Christian Watson**: Reception and yardage over mid-season — he was an explosive WR before injury and the target share is there. Monitor early-season injury updates.
- **Josh Jacobs**: Do not bet props until legal situation resolves.
- **Game totals**: Green Bay home games lean Over early season — offense has enough talent and defense rebuilding under Gannon. Road games off MNF/SNF lean Under given rest disadvantage.
