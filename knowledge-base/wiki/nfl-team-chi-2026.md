## The enormous 2025 luck problem

Chicago's 2025 was arguably one of the luckiest seasons in NFL history:
- **Turnover margin: +22 (#1)** — most takeaways in league (33), fewest giveaways (11). Historical regression data: the 6 prior teams with similar EPA from turnovers (2024 Bills +112.9, 2013 Chiefs +98.6, etc.) all regressed by -6 to -28 EPA next year. None came close to repeating.
- **Fumble luck: #1** — recovered 5.5 more fumbles than expected. Pure variance.
- **Field goal luck: #1**
- **"Bears Tax"**: Opponents had **#1 fewest penalty yards** against Chicago for 4 straight years (2022-2025) — -795 net penalty yards over 4 years, no other team close. Worth ~3 touchdowns per season in hidden EPA. Likely to regress.
- Despite all this, the Bears ranked **#26 in total defensive efficiency**

These luck factors combined likely generated 3-5 extra wins in 2025.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Caleb Williams' stable QB metrics (no-pressure, clean pocket, first downs) all improved dramatically → Year 3 trajectory could unlock accuracy improvements
- **Colston Loveland** is a legitimate top-5 TE weapon: +0.51 EPA/target (6th among all players), matchup nightmare in 2-TE sets
- **Luther Burden III** emerged as a true WR1 in last 5 games of 2025 — with Moore gone, he gets a full year of that opportunity
- O-line climbed to #6 — #1 pass-block win rate in 2025 protects Williams
- **+15 net rest days is #1 in NFL** — structural advantage in key games
- Ben Johnson is elite; his offenses are top-10 in scoring in every season

**Under argument**
- **Turnover margin regression alone** could cost 3-4 wins — the #1 takeaway unit lost Byard, Edmunds, and CJ Gardner-Johnson (the three biggest contributors)
- Bears Tax regression: if opponent penalty yards normalize, Chicago loses ~3 TDs of hidden EPA
- Front 7 is **#30** — pass rush is severely depleted; only Montez Sweat is reliable
- Caleb Williams accuracy hasn't improved in two years; if it stays at 14.6% inaccuracy, efficiency plateaus
- Schedule jumps from 4th easiest → 6th hardest; #1 toughest pass defense schedule
- 11 wins was a ceiling output with maximum luck support

**Sharp lean**: Under. The 2025 Bears stacked #1 fumble luck + #1 turnover margin + #1 field goal luck + Bears Tax + 4th easiest schedule simultaneously. Each alone generates regression. The schedule gets harder. The takeaway unit is gutted. 9.5 is still too high.

## Prop angles

- **Colston Loveland**: Over on receiving yards and TDs — with Ben Johnson's 12-personnel usage and Loveland's elite efficiency, this is a top-3 TE prop in the NFL. Attack early-season lines before market catches up to his role expansion.
- **Luther Burden III**: Under on early-season volume (inconsistent first half of 2025); attack target share props from Week 8+ when his role is confirmed. If he gets 100+ targets, he's a value WR2 for the year.
- **Caleb Williams passing yards/TDs**: Over in favorable matchups if Williams shows accuracy improvement in camp/preseason. The ceiling is real, but the floor is constrained by accuracy.
- **D'Andre Swift**: Volume props under — Ben Johnson's offense generates rushing yards from run-pass balance. Swift is solid but not a 20+ carry back.
- **Defense totals**: Fade Bears in game totals when playing high-powered offenses — Front 7 #30, secondary turnover-dependent.
