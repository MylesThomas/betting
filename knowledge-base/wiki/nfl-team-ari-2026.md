## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Worst injury luck in NFL in 2025 (#32 total player health) → regression = more wins
- Jeremiyah Love + McBride give a real offensive floor
- LaFleur's scheme is better than Keim-era Cardinals chaos

**Under argument**
- Hardest schedule in NFL (#32 SoS)
- QB room is genuinely #32 — no upside unless Beck surprises
- Defense (#29 Front 7, #28 Secondary) is also a bottom-5 unit
- 2025 record: 3-14

**Sharp lean**: Under. Schedule + QB room is too punishing even with injury luck regression. Fade in win total futures.

---

## Prop angles

- **Trey McBride**: Elite target volume (record-breaking 2025). Safe floor for reception and yardage props. Watch for red-zone TD regression (he will get used).
- **Jeremiyah Love**: High rush attempt upside in wide-zone scheme; Cardinals will lean on run to compensate for weak passing game. Bet over rush attempts in favorable matchups.
