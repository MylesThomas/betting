## Tyler Shough: reading the 2025 run correctly

Shough's December hot streak generated enormous optimism. Context required:

**Where he succeeded**: Shough's final 5-game stretch (70% comp, 1,316 yds, 5 TDs, 2 INTs, 135 rushing yds, 3 rushing TDs) came against pass defenses: #28 MIA, #25 TB, #13 CAR, #30 NYJ, #27 TEN — five of the weakest units in the NFL.

**Where he was absent**: He played 7 of 9 starts without Alvin Kamara and 8 without Chris Olave. The Saints also traded Rashid Shaheed mid-season.

**His actual ranking among recent rookies**: Among 28 rookie QBs with 100+ attempts in the past 5 years — he ranked #7 in EPA/att (-0.01). That sounds good; Dart, Maye, Daniels, Stroud, Richardson, Pennix, and Williams all ranked around that cluster. His Rattler comparison makes him look like an upgrade but isn't the full story.

**The high-leverage collapse is real**: His red zone ranking (#30 EPA, #31 success) and 3rd-down ranking (#31 EPA) are worrying for a QB asked to win games against real defenses in 2026.

**Bottom line**: Shough is a legitimate franchise prospect — not a lock. 2026 is the actual test.

## Defense: Brandon Staley's first year

The defense's 2025 improvement was one of the most dramatic in the league:
- 2024: **#30 in total defense** (379.9 yards/game)
- 2025: **#9 in total defense** (299.8 yards/game) — 80 yards/game improvement

Under Staley's scheme:
- **#10 in EPA per play allowed**
- **#7 in success rate allowed**
- **#6 in yards per play**
- **45 sacks (10th in NFL)**

**The anchor losses**: Demario Davis (→ Jets) led team with 143 tackles and forced 2 fumbles; was the most indispensable piece. Cameron Jordan announced retirement, then returned — his status is the biggest wildcard.

**2026 additions**: Kaden Elliss (LB, homecoming — drafted by Saints in 2019, spent 4 years with them, knows the system), Tremaine Edmunds (IDL depth), Christen Miller (2nd round).

**The concern**: The front seven lost its two most experienced leaders (Davis gone, Jordan's age/retirement risk). Bryan Bresee has shown only inconsistent impact in 3 seasons.

## The 2025 offensive disasters

The Saints led the NFL in two uniquely bad categories:

**Red zone + kicking — dead last in both**:
- Offensive RZ TD%: **44.4% (#32)** — worst mark since 2017, #2 worst since 2011
- Goal-to-go TD%: **44.4% (#32)** — NFL average is 74.4%
- Own FG%: **71.4% (#32)** — kicker Blake Grupe fired Week 12
- FGA EPA: -8.25 (#31)

**First quarter — worst in the century**:
- First-quarter point margin: **-93 (#32)** — worst for any team since 2001
- First-quarter PPG: **1.71 (#32)**
- First drive: #32 EPA/play, #32 3-and-out rate, #32 score rate, #31 TD rate

Moore has addressed both: Nussmeier as OC brings a fresh offensive identity, Travis Etienne replaces Kamara in the backfield, and Tyson gives Shough a second receiver.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Tyler Shough Year 2 — even modest improvement in high-leverage situations (RZ, 3rd down) produces more scoring; the weapons around him are now real (Olave + Tyson + Etienne)
- Travis Etienne is a genuine upgrade over Kamara (who was #47/48 in rushing yards over expected despite the lightest boxes of any RB); Etienne's zone-scheme production at JAC was elite
- Schedule is **#2 easiest in NFL** — the NFC South features three teams (Falcons #20, Buccaneers #23, Panthers #29) on tougher roads; New Orleans is the only team with an easier-than-average slate
- Kellen Moore proved himself a competent first-year HC; 2026 is Year 2 with continuity
- Brandon Staley's defense should sustain its improvement; the scheme is intact even without Davis

**Under argument**
- Shough's red zone (#30 EPA) and 3rd-down (#31 EPA) and 4th-quarter (#31 EPA) performance are not flukes — they reflect real deficiencies in a young QB who hasn't faced real defenses regularly
- Front seven is **#28** — the biggest hole on the roster; Jordan's return is not guaranteed and Davis is gone
- RZ efficiency and kicking were historically bad; fixing both simultaneously in Year 1 of a new OC is aggressive to assume
- Turnover margin was -4 (Saints); their defense forced 20 TOs, offense gave away 24 (turnovers are sticky)
- First-quarter margin of -93 is partially scheme (fixable) but partially QB (less fixable immediately)

**Sharp lean (Grade B-)**: Neutral. The Saints overachieved relative to preseason expectations in 2025 and will carry genuine optimism into 2026. 7.5 wins is achievable with the second-easiest schedule if Shough takes a meaningful step forward. The key question is whether the late-2025 hot streak represents a real signal or an easy-schedule artifact.

## Prop angles

- **Chris Olave**: Receptions and yards over — with Shough starting and Tyson drawing double-coverage, Olave's target share should remain elite; 100+ catch season is the floor with a healthy Shough
- **Travis Etienne**: Rush yards over — the scheme finally gets a RB who can exploit blocking (Etienne vs. Kamara is a massive upgrade in zone-scheme fit); but **fade on short-yardage TDs** given his documented #51 EPA in short-yardage situations
- **Tyler Shough**: Passing yards over full-season — Moore will script early-game passes to set a rhythm; the system will throw more than in 2025 with Etienne reducing box counts; but red zone passing TDs fade
- **Jordyn Tyson**: Emergence bet — a high-upside speculative play; if his size/speed translates early, his target share grows rapidly with Olave drawing attention; but the career 1.1% below-expected catch rate needs correction
- **Game totals**: Lean Over early-season home games against NFC South opponents; Lean Under road games vs top-10 defenses given Shough's road/pressure struggles
