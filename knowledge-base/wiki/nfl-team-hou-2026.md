## The 2025 defense: legitimately the best in football

Houston was **#1 in total defense by Sharp's metrics** in 2025:
- Allowed 295 points, **17.4 per game (#2 NFL)**
- **#32 scheme diversity** — the most straightforward defense in football; they trust their pass rushers
- When NOT blitzing: **37% pressure rate (#4 NFL)** — four rushers beating five blockers routinely
- When blitzing: **55% pressure rate (#1 NFL)**
- Anderson + Hunter = possibly the best EDGE tandem in the NFL entering 2026
- Stingley + Lassiter = arguably the best CB duo in the NFL
- Defense trajectory under Ryans: **#30 before arrival → #14 → #5 → #2**

The 2025 season was carried almost entirely by defense. The offense was **#28 in efficiency** and it didn't matter.

## C.J. Stroud: the fork in the road

Stroud's career arc tells a complex story:

| Season | EPA/att | Success Rate | YPA | Comp% |
|--------|---------|-------------|-----|-------|
| 2023 | +0.10 (#6) | 45% | 8.2 | 64% |
| 2024 | -0.04 | 40% | 7.0 | 63% |
| 2025 | +0.06 | 42% | 7.2 | 65% |

He rebounded from 2024 but didn't return to rookie form. Outside the red zone: #22 success, #13 EPA — top half of the league. Inside the red zone: **#29 success, #28 EPA, #41 Y/A, #25 comp%** — catastrophic.

**Playoff performance**: vs Steelers + Patriots: 51.9% comp, 0 TDs, 5 INTs, 6 sacks. The Texans declined to extend him, picking up only his 5th-year option — a clear signal that the front office is "not yet betting on him."

**Red zone scheme issue**: Caley ran 78% of red zone early-down passes out of 3+ WR sets (#4 highest rate). That approach generated terrible results in both LA (while he was with the Rams) and Houston. McVay course-corrected after Sharp's book last year — if Caley makes the same fix, HOU red zone improves significantly.

## The field goal luck problem

In 2025, Houston benefited from **the single best FG luck in the NFL**:
- **Net FG over expectation: +8.7 (#1)**
- Own FG made: 48/52 (92.3%) — excellent
- **Opponents' FG made: 18/27 (66.7%) — #1 lowest rate in the league**

This gifted Houston wins in close games. Regression to the mean on opponent FG% alone is worth ~1-2 wins in the wrong direction in 2026.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Defense is legitimately the **#1 unit in the NFL** — Front 7 #1, Secondary #1
- Schedule eases dramatically: #2 toughest → #7 toughest; rush defense faced goes from #3 to #21 (#1 largest ease)
- David Montgomery is a perfect scheme fit (man-blocking #1 EPA/rush); OL addresses run blocking
- If Caley fixes red zone scheme (McVay model), offense jumps meaningfully
- Stroud has two years left on rookie deal; team structure keeps floor high

**Under argument**
- FG luck regression: +8.7 net in 2025 is one of the most extreme luck figures in recent NFL history; opponents will make FGs at a normal rate in 2026
- OL is **#31** — the second-worst OL in the NFL, despite two straight offseasons of rebuilding; run blocking is still structurally broken
- Stroud had a **playoff meltdown** and the team didn't extend him — internal confidence is lower than public perception
- Receivers are **#11** but lack explosive threats; Tank Dell's health is uncertain; WR room has no legitimate #1 outside Collins
- **Games off road SNF/MNF: #24** — slight rest disadvantage baked in
- The 12-5 record in 2025 required the #1 defense AND the best FG luck in the NFL AND a favorable schedule; all three are regressing

**Sharp lean**: Neutral to slight Over. The defense is real and the schedule gets easier. But 9.5 wins requires Stroud to take a real step forward — not just away from the 2024 floor — because the FG luck and schedule advantages of 2025 aren't coming back.

## Prop angles

- **C.J. Stroud**: Fade TD over and YPA over early season until red zone scheme improves. His outside-RZ numbers are fine (top-half), but red zone TDs (#30 RZ TD%) are a drag on the counting stats. If Caley makes adjustments by midseason, revisit.
- **Will Anderson Jr.**: Sacks over — highest pass-rush win rate in the NFL, extended to be here long-term, elite floor with Hunter drawing double-coverage.
- **Danielle Hunter**: Sacks over — 15 sacks in 2025 from the #7 pressure rate spot; legitimate 15-18 sack ceiling if healthy.
- **Nico Collins**: Target share over — he is the only established pass-catcher on the roster; 120 targets is his floor with a healthy Dell/Higgins splitting second options.
- **David Montgomery**: Rush attempts over early season — he is the clear starter and the team invested heavily in the man-blocking scheme that fits his game; scheme-specific upside.
- **Game totals**: Lean Under on Houston road games vs top-10 offenses early — offense isn't yet fixed; defense may yield more to elite passing teams. Home games lean Under (defense elite, opponent offenses score less).
