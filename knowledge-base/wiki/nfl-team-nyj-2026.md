## The 2025 historic disaster

The Jets finished 3-14 with statistical lows that stand alone:

- **ZERO interceptions** — first team in NFL history to finish a season with 0 INTs; -19 turnover margin (#32)
- **Halftime deficit**: -175 total points — worst cumulative first-half performance since 2000
- **0-13 vs teams with winning records**
- **Justin Fields first-half metrics**: #41 YPA (5.4), #40 EPA/att (-0.21), #38 comp% (61%), #33 success rate (40%); all first-half efficiency ranked #32

The one exception: kicking was genuinely elite (**96.6% FG%, #1**).

---

## The rebuild: selling for picks

Before the 2025 trade deadline, the Jets sold their best defensive players:
- **Sauce Gardner** → Colts: 2 first-round picks + Adonai Mitchell
- **Quinnen Williams** → Cowboys: 2027 1st + 2026 2nd + Mazi Smith

The **#2 overall pick (David Bailey)** and **#16 (Kenyon Sadiq, via Colts)** are the direct return. The organization is explicitly targeting 2027 as the year to compete with accumulated capital.

**The core question for 2026**: Can Glenn survive long enough to execute the rebuild he set in motion? Sharp's tone suggests the front office may not be patient enough.

---

## Geno Smith: bridge with a ceiling

At 36, Smith is explicitly a placeholder while Klubnik develops. The Raiders paying a portion of his salary is a structural acknowledgment that this is a low-commitment bridge. His best career stretch (2022-2023 SEA) showed a functional pocket passer when surrounded with weapons — but he hasn't had an elite supporting cast in years and won't in 2026.

**The Garrett Wilson dependency**: Wilson remains the only legitimate WR1 on this roster. If his knee doesn't fully recover, the receiving corps is among the worst units in the NFL.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- 5th-easiest schedule; Glenn may be surprisingly functional with Bailey providing immediate pass-rush impact
- Minkah Fitzpatrick directly addresses the 0 INT problem — turnover margin regression toward 0 is realistic
- Rest structure is favorable (zero short-week road games, zero games off road SNF/MNF, bye intact)
- Breece Hall is a legitimate RB1 with the ball in his hands (4.4 YPC on 243 carries)
- David Bailey (33.6% pressure rate) can contribute immediately; the front 7 was actually #16, not the weakest unit

**Under argument**
- HC #32 — Glenn is the lowest-rated coach in Sharp's rankings; trust the panel
- QB #30 — Geno Smith at 36 isn't a winning starter; Klubnik is unproven
- Secondary #24 after trading Sauce Gardner (the most credentialed corner in franchise history)
- Garrett Wilson's knee status going into training camp is the entire offense
- 2027 rebuild posture means the organization has limited incentive to sacrifice long-term assets for a few more 2026 wins
- -19 turnover margin in 2025 was historically bad; even half-regression is a significant ask

**Sharp lean (Grade D)**: The Jets are explicitly a rebuilding franchise oriented around 2027. Glenn's coaching rating (#32) combined with a bridge QB and a traded defensive core makes 5.5 wins a number that requires everything to go right. The schedule is easy enough to generate 5-6 wins, but the roster has too many holes at QB, WR, and secondary to exceed expectations.

## Prop angles

- **Breece Hall**: Rush yards over — the OL (#19) is acceptable and Hall is the entire offense when Geno can't carry the team; he'll see enormous touch volume
- **David Bailey**: Sacks over early-season — elite college pressure rate (33.6%) translates immediately; the Jets will let him pin his ears back as the obvious bright spot
- **Garrett Wilson**: Health-gated — if confirmed healthy Week 1, targets over; without him, the entire passing game collapses; do not bet full-season props until training camp confirmation
- **Kenyon Sadiq**: Emergence bet — if Wilson misses time, Sadiq becomes the primary receiving option early in his career; an early-usage spike is possible
- **Game totals**: Lean Under in road games where Geno Smith must carry a one-score deficit
