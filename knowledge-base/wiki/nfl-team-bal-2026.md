## The Lamar Jackson health variable

Pre-hamstring 2025 (Wks 1-3): 9.1 Y/A (#1), +0.26 EPA/att (#3), 72% comp (#5), 11% TD rate (#1), 25% explosive rate (#1)
Post-hamstring 2025 (Wks 9+): -0.07 EPA/att (#33), 60% comp, 5% TD rate (#11), 11.6 TD:INT ratio (#27)

Historical rushing yards per game: consistently 50+ yards every prior season → fell to **26.9 in 2025** (13th). If he's healthy, this is a different team.

The swing between a healthy Lamar and an injured Lamar is among the largest single-player impact gaps in the NFL.

**Key game model — Lamar designed runs per game:**
| Designed Runs | Career W-L | Win% | Last 3 Yrs | Win% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7+ | 35-9 | **80%** | 7-1 | **88%** |
| 6 or fewer | 44-27 | 62% | 26-16 | 62% |

The Ravens' win probability swings ~20-26 points based purely on Lamar's designed run usage. Watch this in-game: if Jackson is running early, tilt live bets toward Baltimore.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Lamar Jackson's hamstring injuries were bad luck — missed only 1 game in prior 2 seasons. If healthy, he reverts to #1-tier QB efficiency
- Schedule drops from 12th-hardest to 6th-easiest — massive improvement
- Jesse Minter is a strong defensive coordinator hire; Baltimore defense has talent
- Minter's Chargers defense allowed 6th-lowest opp EPA/play in 2025 despite injury-riddled rosters
- New defensive additions (Hendrickson, Campbell) add pass rush depth that was clearly missing

**Under argument**
- Lamar is 30 with a long injury history; his rushing production — the unique multiplier — may be diminished
- Derrick Henry entering age-32 season; could decline sharply
- O-Line lost Linderbaum (center) — hard to replace
- Receivers unit is #25 — no genuine outside speed threat
- HC Jesse Minter is unproven as a head coach; scheme will shift
- Ravens went 0-5 in 1-score games in 2025; 0-2 in OT; 0% win rate in field-goal-margin games

**Sharp lean**: Over. The schedule improvement + Lamar health regression-to-the-mean is a strong prior. 11.5 is high but the talent + coaching is legitimate. The key bet: watch Week 1-3 health; if Lamar looks healthy and running, attack overs.

## Prop angles

- **Lamar Jackson rushing**: Monitor closely — if healthy, his rushing yards/att props will be underpriced given books will anchor on 2025 numbers. His healthy-Lamar baseline is 50+ rush yards per game.
- **Lamar Jackson passing**: In injury-shortened 2025 he dropped to #33 in EPA/att. If fully healthy, expect reversion toward top-5 efficiency. Over on passing TDs, yards.
- **Derrick Henry**: Fade volume props if he shows age-related decline early. His YPC (5.2) was real but 300 carries at 32 is a red flag. Watch for snap share to RB2 creeping up.
- **Zay Flowers**: Limited big-play profile; target share props are safer than yardage props. Under on receiving yards in difficult matchups.
