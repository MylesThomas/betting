## The 2025 disaster: what actually happened

The Raiders went 3-14, but the roster problems were structural and largely correctable:

**OL was historically bad**:
- **0.5 yards before contact per rush — worst by any team since 2013**
- #32 yards per carry, #32 EPA/rush, #32 success rate, #32 stuff rate
- Jackson Powers-Johnson played only 8 games (ankle); Bradley Bozeman graded dead last among centers (30 pressures allowed)
- Most-used OL combination was on the field for only 23.5% of offensive snaps

**Jeanty was not the problem**:
- 54% of runs contacted at or behind LOS (#3 highest in NFL) — scheme exposure, not back failure
- Managed 3.06 yards after contact (middle of pack for high-volume backs)
- 1,321 yards and 10 TDs despite one of the worst rushing environments in modern NFL history

**Geno Smith was the problem**:
- -0.12 EPA/att, 40% success rate, 17 INTs (only player in NFL with 15+), 64 sacks allowed (#1 in NFL)
- 10.9% sack rate (#40 of qualifying QBs)
- Accuracy was actually elite historically (led NFL in completion% over expected 2021-2024) — the OL exposed him
- Pete Carroll inserted himself into play-calling and DC decisions; fired Jan 5 after the season

**Brock Bowers was not the problem**:
- Played 12 hobbled games and still posted 64/680/7
- 43% of targets were in the red zone; TE target rank #2 despite the offense being historically bad

## The rebuild plan: Mendoza + Kubiak + Linderbaum

The 2026 plan is explicit: develop Fernando Mendoza under Kubiak with a functional OL.

**Mendoza** (Indiana, #1 pick):
- 27 TDs, 0 INTs in red zone in college; led Hoosiers to 16-0 national title
- 6'5", precision pocket passer; Kubiak covets his size, poise, and decision-making
- Threw 0 interceptions in the red zone — directly addresses the team's #1 weakness from 2025

**Kubiak's system** is tailor-made for the two healthy stars on the roster:
- Wide-zone running scheme: creates running lanes after contact, exactly where Jeanty excels
- Heavy TE involvement: Bowers becomes the center of the passing game in play-action concepts

**Linderbaum** gives the offense a foundation it has lacked since at least 2013. The Raiders averaged 0.5 yards before contact per rush — Linderbaum provides the zone-blocking communication a Kubiak scheme demands.

**Cousins** as bridge: peaked under Kubiak in Minnesota (2021: 33 TDs); can credibly start Week 1 while Mendoza develops, and serves as an ideal film/system study partner.

## Defensive outlook

Rob Leonard (promoted from DLine coach) installs a Mike Macdonald-style defense:
- 3-4 base (57%), Nickel (35%)
- Rush 3 efficiency: #4 (elite); Rush 5: #31 (poor) — structure demands clean 4-man rush
- DLine: Jonah Laulu, Adam Butler, Thomas Booker; Maxx Crosby still on roster
- CB: Eric Stokes (1st season in LV — fewest yards allowed per coverage snap among qualified corners); Taron Johnson (nickel slot); Darien Porter; Jermod McCoy (4th round, TEN — injured)
- LB: Quay Walker, Nakobe Dean — both added to address run defense
- S: Jeremy Chinn (combo corner/safety; 9th among DBs in run stuff rank), Treydan Stukes (2nd round)
- **2025 defense** gave up 6.0 yards per pass attempt (#9 — not bad), surrendered fewest YPC to WRs (#2); the secondary was better than the record suggests

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Luck regression: 3-14 overstates true talent; OL was the proximate cause of nearly every failure
- Linderbaum directly fixes the worst rushing environment since 2013 — even an upgrade to average unlocks Jeanty
- Kubiak's scheme maximizes both healthy stars (Bowers, Jeanty)
- "A realistic expectation in 2026 would be doubling last season's win total" — 6-7 wins is Over 5.5
- Rest structure is the best in the NFL (#1 no short-week road, #1 no prime-time road drain)
- No prime-time games means no public betting market overreaction on a rebuilding team

**Under argument**
- AFC West is the toughest division in football; favored in only 3 games
- WR room is **#29** — the most glaring roster hole; without a No. 1 receiver, Mendoza's development has a ceiling
- Mendoza is a rookie QB; Cousins is an aging bridge option; QB uncertainty is real
- The defense ranked #27 Front 7, #30 Secondary — unit rankings are genuinely poor
- Allegiant Stadium is a visitor-friendly venue (70% opposition fans on game days); no real home-field advantage

**Sharp lean**: Slight Over. The organizational direction is clear for the first time in years. Linderbaum + Kubiak + a healthy OL unlocks both Jeanty and Bowers. 5.5 wins requires the team to stay broken — the 2025 was uniquely catastrophic in a correctable way.

## Prop angles

- **Ashton Jeanty**: Rush attempts over — the team is built around him and he handled 84.3% of backfield touches in a historically bad environment; scheme upgrade should produce 1,400+ yard pace. YPC over is live if OL improves even modestly.
- **Brock Bowers**: Receptions/targets over — he is the focal point of Kubiak's heavy-TE system; red zone target share over is particularly interesting (43% of his targets were already in RZ in 2025).
- **Fernando Mendoza**: Fade in Year 1 props — rookie QBs on rebuilding teams rarely post efficient counting stats; let him establish before betting.
- **Kirk Cousins**: Fade completions/YPA in first half of season — Kubiak run-first identity; Cousins' value is as system anchor, not stat accumulator.
- **Game totals**: Lean Under in LV road games vs top-10 pass defenses — the offense will be run-first and the WR room (#29) limits the passing ceiling; Unders have structural value in a low-pressure rebuild year.
