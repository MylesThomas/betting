## The 2025 collapse: 15-2 to 6-11

Kansas City went from the best team in the NFL to one of the worst in one season. The annotated breakdown:

**Mahomes individual decline** (before the ACL/LCL tear):
- Career-worst in: completion rate (62.7%), accuracy, sack+INT rate, TD rate, 3rd-down conversion
- First-quarter scoring decline: **2021 #1 → 2022 #2 → 2023 #12 → 2024 #16 → 2025 #28**
- Pre-snap motion: **#25 usage, #26 efficiency in 2025** (was #10 usage, #13 efficiency in 2024)
- Play action: **#30 usage in 2025** (was elite: #8 YA, #3 success rate in prior 3 years combined)

**Historic bad luck in close games**:
- **1-score games 2025: 1-9 (.100, #32)** — historically terrible
- In 2024: 11-0 in one-score games (#1 NFL)
- **Opponents made 96.9% of FG attempts against KC** — the best opponent FG% of any team in the NFL in 2025; historically extreme bad luck

**Schedule/opposition**:
- **0-8 against playoff teams**
- **0-8 against winning-record teams**

## The Mahomes injury and 2026 outlook

Mahomes tore ACL + LCL in Week 15 of 2025. He was cleared for dropbacks and throwing sessions by early 2026. The expectation is a full return for the 2026 season.

The early schedule (Weeks 1-4, #1 easiest) gives Mahomes a soft landing to shake off rust before facing tougher opponents after the bye.

**Key question**: Is the pre-snap motion and play-action decline a scheme problem (Nagy era) or a Mahomes regression? Bieniemy's return as OC is a direct bet that the problem was schematic.

---

## Kelce erosion

Travis Kelce's target efficiency has declined each year since his 2022 peak:
- **Success rate**: #22 in 2025
- **Yards per target**: #20 in 2025
- The counting stats have held up largely because of volume; the underlying metrics say he is no longer an elite TE asset

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- **Luck regression alone should add 4-5 wins**: 1-9 in one-score games and opponents making 96.9% of FGs are both historically extreme and not repeatable
- Mahomes with a healthy OL and Bieniemy restoring pre-snap motion/play action should look more like the 2024 version
- Weeks 1-4 (#1 easiest) + Week 5 bye = Mahomes can ease back with maximum recovery runway
- Pass defense faced: #32 easiest — the schedule structurally inflates defensive performance
- Kenneth Walker III addresses a real run game weakness; explosive back fits Reid's system
- DLine health ranked #1, Front 7 returns mostly intact

**Under argument**
- McDuffie and Watson both left for the Rams; Delane (rookie) has to replace two cornerbacks immediately
- Secondary is now #25 — from elite to below average in one offseason
- Kelce erosion is real and structural; no elite TE option to replace the production curve
- Pre-snap motion and play-action declines may not be entirely scheme-driven; Mahomes at age 31 post-ACL+LCL is an unknown
- WR room is #20 with no proven top-end talent
- Post-bye schedule is #9 toughest — the cupcake start doesn't last

**Sharp lean**: Neutral to slight Over. The luck normalization in one-score games and FG differential alone implies 10-11 wins in a normal year. But secondary regression, Kelce erosion, and a post-ACL Mahomes are real risks on the downside. 10.5 wins is a reasonable line — the juice determines the bet.

## Prop angles

- **Patrick Mahomes**: Fade early-season stats (Weeks 1-4 cupcake schedule inflates numbers). Wait for Week 6+ before trusting prop prices. TD over is live on a full-season basis if he returns healthy — but wait for confirmation.
- **Kenneth Walker III**: Rush attempts over — he is the clear RB1 in a system that wants to run; explosive metrics translate well to Reid's scheme. YPC over is interesting early when the schedule is #1 easiest.
- **Travis Kelce**: Receptions under relative to his peak lines — target efficiency declining every year; don't pay for the name. Reception prop should price below 2023-24 comps.
- **Mansoor Delane**: INTs over for a full season — #6 pick corners tend to see immediate volume; will face baptism-by-fire opposite a weaker CB2.
- **Game totals**: Lean Over in KC home games Weeks 1-4 — Mahomes returning to health against bottom-tier defenses; Over 1H in these games is higher-value than full-game given conservative Reid late-game management.
