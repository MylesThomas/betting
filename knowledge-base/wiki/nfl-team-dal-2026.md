# Dallas Cowboys 2026

**Sharp forecast**: 9.5 wins — **Division Rank #2 (NFC East)**

---

## Coaching & scheme

- HC: **Brian Schottenheimer** (Year 2; HC rank #23; promoted from OC after offense was #2 most efficient in 2025)
- New OC: Klayton Adams (run-game specialist; architect of Javonte Williams' revival)
- New DC: **Christian Parker** (34; never been DC at any level; from Fangio passing-game coordinator tree under Vic Fangio at PHI)

## The Micah Parsons saga

On March 5, Maxx Crosby signed a $35.5M/yr deal, which set the EDGE market. By March 14, Myles Garrett signed for $40M/yr. When Danielle Hunter signed for $35.6M in late March, Parsons still had no deal.

Jerry Jones invited Parsons to "chat" at The Star in Frisco. Parsons left convinced a handshake deal was done. Jones viewed it as non-binding brainstorming. His agent rejected the proposed contract. On August 1, Parsons formally requested a trade. He was dealt to the Packers on August 28 — days before kickoff — leaving new HC Schottenheimer and first-year DC Eberflus (later fired) zero runway to rebuild the defense around his absence.

Result: **511 points allowed** — the most in the 66-year history of the Cowboys franchise. #32 in total defensive efficiency, #32 in red zone defense, #31 in pass defense. The Cowboys went 7-10 despite being the #2 most efficient offense playing the #2 easiest schedule.

## The 2025 offense: elite

Prescott's transformation under the play-action heavy scheme was staggering:
- Career (2020-2024): +35.7 EPA on play-action over **568 dropbacks** (avg +0.06/att)
- 2025 alone: +51.9 EPA on play-action over **173 dropbacks** (+0.30/att)

He ranked #9 in the NFL when passing clean — up from #34 in 2024. #3 from the pocket (was #29). With Lamb and Pickens drawing DPI at league-leading rates, Dallas manufactured free chunk yardage that doesn't appear in receiving stats. They combined for the #1 and #2 DPI totals in the NFL (121 and 120 yards drawn respectively through Week 17).

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Receivers are **#1 in the NFL** — Lamb + Pickens is the best WR duo in football; untouched by offseason
- Prescott's play-action transformation was scheme-driven and Adams stays as OC; 67.3% comp + 30 TDs + 7.6 YPA baseline
- Defense has actual plan now: Parker's Fangio system, Caleb Downs as FS anchor, Quinnen Williams, Rashan Gary — all fit schematically
- Rest edges compound in back half: +10 net from Week 13 on; 4 advantages vs 1 disadvantage in playoff race crunch time
- Javonte Williams is proven as a 4.8 YPC back in this scheme; run game floor is established

**Under argument**
- Schedule jumps from **#2 easiest to #4 hardest** — the **#1 largest increase in difficulty** in the NFL; the offense now faces real defenses
- Defense must go from worst in franchise history (#32, 511 pts) to functional, under a **34-year-old first-year DC** who has never called plays at any level
- Parsons is in Green Bay; LB unit is still #30 (worst in NFL at LB)
- Chronic penalty problem: #1 most penalized team in 5 of past 6 seasons; persists across coaching changes; 133 accepted penalties, 1,136 yards
- O-Line slipped to #18 from 16th; injuries to Guyton and Beebe forced constant shuffling — 6+ different OL combinations in 2025
- The Cowboys trailed in 16 of 17 games despite a top-2 offense — that's a signal of systemic dysfunction

**Sharp lean**: Neutral. The offense is legitimately elite and the rest edge structure is favorable. But the schedule jump is the biggest in the NFL and the defense is being rebuilt under a complete novice coordinator with no experience. Dallas can be a 9-10 win team or a 7-8 win team depending entirely on whether Parker's defense becomes functional.

## Prop angles

- **CeeDee Lamb**: Target share and receiving yards over — even in a down health year he had 1,077 yards. If healthy, 1,400+ is realistic against #4 toughest schedule (elite WRs produce against good defenses too).
- **George Pickens**: Over on receptions and yds — career highs in Year 1 with Prescott; man-coverage dominance is scheme-independent. The #1 man-coverage WR in the NFL.
- **Dak Prescott**: Play-action touchdowns over — the scheme is intact; Adams calls the plays; Prescott's clean pocket and play-action splits are top-5. Sack props: under on sack count given improved OL coaching (Riley) and Guyton healthy.
- **Javonte Williams**: Rush attempts over in divisional home games — he's now a true workhorse; Adams' run game philosophy makes Williams a lock for 200+ touches.
- **Game totals**: Dallas home games lean Over early season — offense is elite and new DC Parker needs time to install scheme. Fade Dallas defense in Weeks 1-6 while the Fangio system is being learned.
- **LB props**: Fade any Cowboys LB production props — unit is rated #30; linebackers are hybrid players who may be miscast.
