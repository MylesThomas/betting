## Joe Burrow health history

Burrow has missed significant time in 3 of 6 NFL seasons:
- 2025: Grade 3 turf toe — surgery Week 2; returned Thanksgiving; played only 8 games
- 2025 in those 8 starts: 5-3; +0.08 EPA/att, 67% comp, 7.0 YPA, 17 TDs, 5 INTs
- Without Burrow in 2025: Jake Browning 0-3 (3 starts), Joe Flacco 1-5 (6 starts; averaged 27+ PPG but couldn't close)
- The 2026 forecast (9.5 wins, favored in 15 games) is built almost entirely on Burrow staying healthy

## The Dexter Lawrence transformation

The Bengals used the #10 pick (acquired from Giants) to trade for Dexter Lawrence — the most impactful defensive move of the offseason. The split data is stark:

| Metric | Lawrence ON field | Lawrence OFF field |
|--------|-------------------|--------------------|
| Y/A | #8 | #32 |
| Pressure rate | #15 | #23 |
| Pass success rate | #7 | #26 |
| EPA/pass | #10 | #27 |
| Sack rate | #9 | #31 |

Cincinnati's defense in 2025 ranked **#32 in yards per play** — historically bad. Lawrence alone should move them from 32nd toward the middle of the league on passing downs.

---

## The O-Line structural flaw

The offensive line has ranked 27+ in pass-block win rate for **5 consecutive seasons** and is still rated **#28** in 2026. The Bengals did not sign a single offensive lineman in free agency and did not use a top-100 pick on the OL in the 2026 draft. Burrow's injury history (turf toe, wrist, prior knee) is directly connected to protection.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Joe Burrow is a top-3 QB when healthy — this is the same guy who put up 4,918 yards and 43 TDs in his last healthy season
- Schedule improves from #20 → **#3 easiest** — the competition drop-off is real; only 5 games vs. winning-record teams
- Dexter Lawrence transforms the defense; was carrying Giants' pass defense single-handedly
- Ja'Marr Chase (with Higgins healthy) is the best WR1-WR2 tandem in the AFC
- 0 short-week road games — cleanest schedule structure in the NFL
- Chase Brown is a legitimate 3-down back; offense has depth

**Under argument**
- O-Line is still **#28** — Burrow took 272 hits/hurries in 2025 even before the season-ending injury; structural flaw unaddressed
- 6-11 → 15 wins favored is an enormous leap; market is pricing in a massive bounce-back
- Defense was #32 in yards/play; Lawrence helps but Mafe + Allen + Cook can't rebuild a #32 defense to competitive overnight
- Burrow's injury cycle: missed meaningful time in 3 of 6 seasons; health risk is not small
- Front 7 is still #26; secondary is #23 — outside of Lawrence, the defensive roster is thin

**Sharp lean**: Neutral to slight Over — heavily health-dependent. If Burrow plays 16+ games, the schedule + talent level supports 10+ wins. If he misses 4+ games again, they're a 7-8 win team. This is a binary bet on Burrow health.

## Prop angles

- **Joe Burrow passing stats**: Aggressive over if he clears training camp healthy — the schedule is #3 easiest and Chase/Higgins are elite weapons. Watch preseason injury reports closely before betting.
- **Ja'Marr Chase**: Target share and receiving yards over — Chase was at 185 targets in Burrow's active games; that pace against an easy schedule is elite. Fade if Burrow misses camp time.
- **Chase Brown**: Rush attempts over — he's locked into the starter role with no competition. Volume is safer than efficiency props given #28 OL.
- **Dexter Lawrence defensive props**: Sacks and pressures over — his Giants data proves he's elite even on a bad team. In a real defense with complementary pieces, his production floor is safe.
- **Game totals**: Lean Over in Bengals home games vs. bad defenses early in season. Front 7 is still #26 — fade the defense in totals against good offenses.
