## Josh Allen 2025 — the sack problem

Allen's sack numbers were career-worst in 2025, driven by lack of downfield receivers:
- Sack rate: **15.1%** (career worst; was 4.6% in 2024)
- Sack rate on pressures: **32.2%** (career worst; was 9.8% in 2024)
- Blitz rate on 3rd down: **38.9%** (career high; teams blitzed freely with no downfield threat)
- Average target depth trending down: 9.2 → 8.7 → 8.3 → **7.3** (2022-2025)

D.J. Moore directly addresses this. When teams can't blitz freely, Allen's sack rate reverts to historical 5-10%.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Josh Allen is #1 QB in NFL; when protected, he is top-2 in EPA/att historically
- D.J. Moore reuniting with Joe Brady should fix the downfield threat problem and normalize Allen's sack rate
- James Cook is elite; O-line is top-3; run game gives offense a floor
- Easy schedule (#4 SoS) — favorable matchups throughout
- 62-18 (.775) with no rest disadvantage since 2020 (Wk 6+ threshold)

**Under argument**
- HC Joe Brady is 36 with zero HC experience — huge unknown (HC rank #28)
- 3 short-week road games (historically Bills -9.4% ROI in these spots)
- 4 games in 17 days (Wks 10-13) is brutal; ends with KC in Wk 13 deciding AFC seeding
- Front 7 is #23 — pass rush thinned after losing Bosa, Epenesa; Chubb's health is uncertain
- Receivers still #20 despite Moore; Coleman/Knox remain boom-or-bust
- Bills went just 5-3 in 1-score games (Allen's recurring clutch problem in close games)

**Sharp lean**: Neutral to slight Under. The talent is there (10.5 is accurate for their floor) but the coaching change risk + the schedule traps (3 short-week road games) create real downside. Key spots to **fade**: Weeks 6, 10, 16 road games; Week 13 vs KC.

## Prop angles

- **Josh Allen passing**: With Moore as a true WR1, Allen's target depth and TD rate should recover. Over on passing yards (3,668 was below his norm) and TDs. Watch for lower sack totals once blitz rate normalizes.
- **James Cook**: Elite over value on rush attempts and yards — 1,621 yds was NFL-leading, Cook is locked into this role. Only concern: Brady might pass more with Moore, eating into Cook's volume.
- **D.J. Moore**: Target share over props. With Brady/Allen, he is a true WR1 for the first time since his 2023 Carolina peak. Fade volume props early until role is established; attack mid-season when target share is confirmed.
- **Khalil Shakir**: Aggressive under on any air yards or deep target props — he was historically bad at creating separation downfield. Safe slot receiver only.
