## Jalen Hurts: the 2025 regression

The year-over-year collapse in Hurts' stable metrics is the central story:

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 |
|--------|------|------|
| No Pressure EPA/att | 0.50 (#2) | 0.25 (#17) |
| In Pocket EPA/att | 0.17 (#7) | 0.06 (#19) |
| Play Action EPA/att | 0.30 (#7) | 0.05 (#21) |
| Man coverage EPA/att | 0.36 (#3) | 0.09 (#12) |
| 2-High coverage EPA/att | 0.00 (#22) | -0.09 (**#32**) |

**The 2-High problem**: Hurts ranked dead last in the NFL against 2-High coverage in 2025. Defenses that run Cover 2 or Cover 4 shell concepts have a clear blueprint.

**The running man**: When Hurts carries 10+ times, the Eagles went 29-8 all-time with 10 straight wins entering 2026. But the 2025 offense tried to make Hurts a pure passer (3.3 rushes/gm, career low) — the system worked against them. Mannion's hire is explicitly a correction toward a scheme that fits Hurts' dual-threat identity.

**The QBR collapse**: 55.2 QBR is a career low. The Kevin Patullo-led offense ranked #19 in EPA/play, #19 efficiency, #20 success in 2025 — a dramatic fall from top-10 in 2024.

## The defense: genuinely elite

Despite the offensive regression, the defense was one of the best units in the NFL:
- **14 TD passes allowed (#1 fewest in NFL)**
- **14.9 points/game from Week 10 onward (best in NFL)**
- Quinyon Mitchell + Cooper DeJean: both earned 1st-team All-Pro in 2025 — an unprecedented pairing
- Greenard acquisition gives the front a legitimate star pass rusher
- Zack Baun Pro Bowl performance at LB cemented the middle
- **Off Red Zone TD%: 70.5% (#1)** — best in NFL at scoring when inside the 20

---

## The penalty disaster

In 2025, the Eagles were the most penalized team in the NFL by impact:
- **Penalty margin: -26 (#32)**
- **Defensive penalty EPA: -24.20 (#32)**
- **Net penalty EPA: -23.62 (#31)**
- Accepted penalties: 117 (#22)
- Total penalty yards: 1,073 (#28)

This came in large part from defensive penalties — Fangio's aggressive coverage scheme generated flag exposure. Mannion's hire is unrelated to the defensive penalty problem; fixing it requires scheme adjustment from Fangio.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Mannion's Shanahan-tree scheme unlocks Hurts as a dual-threat — the "10+ carries" split (29-8 all-time) is the blueprint he was hired to execute
- OL (#2) with Barkley in an outside-zone scheme creates the most dangerous rushing attack in the NFC
- Defense is genuinely top-5 in the NFL; with Greenard added to Mitchell + DeJean + Baun + Davis, the unit should improve
- 7th-easiest schedule by opponent quality — the road is manageable
- Only 7 INTs thrown in 2025 (#1 fewest) — turnover discipline is structurally embedded, not luck

**Under argument**
- Net rest **#31** is a genuine structural drag — -22 days deficit in first half of season
- A.J. Brown is gone; Hurts has never operated his best without a true alpha WR1; Makai Lemon is unproven
- **2-High weakness (#32)** is now the league blueprint, same as Cover 6 was for Maye; every DC saw it
- Penalty margin #32 cost multiple possessions per game; structural defensive penalty exposure remains under Fangio
- **FG%: 74.1% (#31)** — kicking is a quiet weakness; the Eagles left points on the field repeatedly
- 4th quarter scoring margin: -36 (#26) — cannot hold leads consistently
- Sirianni HC rank fell from #3 → #14 — the coaching panel has lost confidence; revolving OC door is disruptive for any QB

**Sharp lean (Grade C+)**: "The Eagles do not need to rediscover their talent. They need a coach who stops hiding it." Neutral-to-slight-Under. The talent is real (OL #2, Front 7 #2, Secondary #6, Receivers #8), but the rest disadvantage, the A.J. Brown hole, and Hurts' 2-High vulnerability against increasingly sophisticated defensive schemes make 10.5 a difficult number. The defense alone keeps this team competitive.

## Prop angles

- **Saquon Barkley**: Rush yards over — Mannion's outside zone system maximizes Barkley's patience/vision; the "runs outside zone more than ever" directive is a prop gift; his 2024 season (2,005 yds) showed what this OL can do; fade short-yardage TDs where the scheme over-commits to the outside
- **DeVonta Smith**: Targets and yards over — as the only established WR after Brown's departure, Smith becomes Hurts' primary read on early downs; expect a career high in target share
- **Jalen Hurts**: Rushing yards over — Mannion was specifically hired to get Hurts running again; 10+ carries/game is the explicit goal; but injury risk is real; don't overcommit to long-season props
- **Makai Lemon**: Speculative emergence — if he proves useful early, target his numbers before the market adjusts
- **Team totals**: Lean Over in early-season home games when rest deficit hasn't compounded; Lean Under road games in the first half of season when -22 rest days are biting
