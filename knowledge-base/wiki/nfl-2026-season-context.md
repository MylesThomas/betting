## League-wide landscape

- 2025 Super Bowl: Seahawks (+6000) beat Patriots (+8000) — largest longshot winners in modern history
- 2026 is wide open: **13 teams** at ≤18:1 to win the SB (vs only 5 last year: Chiefs, Eagles, Bills, Ravens, Lions)
- Massive coaching turnover entering 2026: **10 new head coaches, 21 new offensive coordinators, 16 new defensive coordinators** (47 total at those three positions across 32 teams)

Why this matters for betting: when coaches, coordinators, and offensive systems are new, sportsbooks are less calibrated. Their opening lines are based on less historical precedent for that team. This is historically when Sharp sees the most edge.

### Former powers that collapsed in 2025

The top-9 winningest teams 2020–2024 (Chiefs, Bills, Packers, Ravens, Eagles, Bucs, Steelers, Cowboys, Vikings) almost all fell off in 2025:
- Chiefs, Ravens, Bucs, Cowboys, Vikings missed the playoffs and had losing records
- Only the Bills won a playoff game (Wild Card vs Jaguars)
- All nine teams had major coaching changes entering 2026

## 2026 Strength of Schedule

**Methodology**: Use Vegas projected win totals — NOT prior-year win-loss records. Prior-year records explained only **3.9%** of actual SOS in recent years (p = 0.79 — no relationship). Prior-year records are contaminated by luck (fumble recoveries, tipped passes, red-zone variance) that doesn't repeat.

### Easiest schedules in 2026
1. Detroit Lions
2. New Orleans Saints
3. Cincinnati Bengals
4. Cleveland Browns
5. New York Jets

### Hardest schedules in 2026
1. Arizona Cardinals
2. Miami Dolphins
3. Carolina Panthers
4. Dallas Cowboys
5. Los Angeles Rams

**Rule**: Teams with easy schedules (top-8) are far more likely to finish with winning records; teams with hard schedules (bottom-8) are far more likely to finish with losing records. Fade hard-schedule teams in futures and team win totals.

## 2026 NFL Draft Capital (DCOE)

**Metric**: DCOE = Draft Capital Over Expectation. Compares actual draft pick position vs consensus expected position (per Ben Robinson mock drafts + Arif Hasan big boards). Negative DCOE = team got value (drafted later than consensus expected = stole players). Positive DCOE = team overpaid (reached).

### Most valuable 2026 drafts (stole picks)
1. Washington Commanders (DCOE: -1.7)
2. Carolina Panthers
3. Indianapolis Colts
4. Cincinnati Bengals
5. New York Jets

### Least valuable 2026 drafts (reached)
1. San Francisco 49ers (DCOE: +3.1)
2. Los Angeles Rams (+2.2)
3. Houston Texans (+2.2)
4. Jacksonville Jaguars (+1.9)
5. Arizona Cardinals (+1.9)

**Rule**: High positive DCOE teams (49ers, Rams) should be faded in futures because their draft class won't produce as expected. High negative DCOE teams (Commanders, Panthers) have upside in year-2+ player props for those rookies.
