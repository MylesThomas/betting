## The 2025 luck case: historically unsustainable

The 2025 Broncos went 14-3 by setting NFL records in close-game variance:

| Stat | 2025 Broncos | NFL Record context |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| One-score game record | **11-2 (.846)** | Best in NFL 2025 |
| Games trailed at any point | **15 of 17** | Set NFL record for most trailing games ever for a 14-win team |
| Win% when trailing at any point | **80% (12-3)** | #1 NFL |
| Win% when trailing at halftime | **71% (5-2)** | #1 NFL |
| 4th quarter point margin | **+64** | #2 NFL |

In 2024, the same roster went **1-6 in one-score games** (14% win rate). The 2025 Broncos flipped to 11-2. No team in NFL history had ever won 11+ games while trailing in 15 games.

Additional luck headwinds:
- Opponents made field goals at **94.9%** (31st — gifting Denver opponents who kicked poorly)
- Fumble luck: -2.9 net vs expectation (#25)
- Defense played **#26 toughest schedule of offenses**; just THREE rushing offenses ranked better than 15th all season

## Bo Nix and the offense problem

Denver went 14-3 despite their offense being deeply dysfunctional:

**The alignment tell**: When Nix was in shotgun, Denver passed 79% of the time. Under center: 29% pass. That **50-point swing is tied for #1 in the NFL** — any defensive coordinator could exploit this.

**Early-down efficiency**: #23 overall; second-down ranks of #27 yards/play (4.6), #21 success rate — described as "down around the Giants, Raiders, and Panthers."

**Nix's statistical profile**: 63% comp, 6.4 YPA (#28), 13.5% of dropbacks lost 1.0 EPA or worse (#2 worst in NFL, only Bryce Young worse), ranked #30 QB overall.

The team went 14-3 despite this because the defense was elite and the 4th quarter margin was +64.

**Key change**: Payton handed play-calling to Davis Webb for the first time in 17 years. Webb's Buffalo background suggests more aggressive, downfield passing. The question is whether Nix can handle that aggression or whether more ambition creates more turnovers.

## The elite defense

The 2025 Denver defense was legitimately excellent:
- **#2 success rate**, **#2 early-down defense**, **#1 yards per play allowed** (4.5), **#7 EPA per play**
- **#1 sacks per pass attempt** (68 sacks!), **#3 pressure rate**
- **#1 TD rate forced per drive**; **#3 points per drive allowed** (1.62)
- Patrick Surtain: 2 receptions at 10+ yards allowed in 296 snaps — historic
- Nucleus returns intact: Surtain, Bonitto, Cooper, Allen, Joseph (LB)
- Lost Franklin-Myers (EDGE) but added Tyler Onyedim (3rd round IDL)

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Defense is legitimately elite and returns its top-10 defenders and 17 of top 20 snaps
- O-Line is **#1 in NFL** — Garett Bolles, elite protection; allowed only 23 sacks in 2025 (fewest in NFL)
- Dobbins healthy + Coleman = real run game; OL creates yards before contact at elite rate
- Jaylen Waddle adds explosiveness that was dead last in the NFL
- Schedule is 11th easiest; 5 games with rest advantage
- Davis Webb's offensive aggression could unlock Nix's ceiling

**Under argument**
- One-score luck regression: went 11-2 in one-score games; expected regression to ~6-7 (.500) alone costs 4-5 wins; this alone makes 9.5 wins unreachable at the median
- Schedule jump: rush defense faced goes from #29 to **#2 hardest** (biggest change in NFL); pass defenses also get harder
- Bo Nix has shown no ability to win games with his arm; at 6.4 YPA and with a massive alignment tell, he is structurally limited
- Davis Webb has never called NFL plays; handing the keys to a 31-year-old first-time OC is a significant gamble
- Receivers were dead last in explosiveness in 2025; Waddle helps but the system was the problem as much as the personnel

**Sharp lean**: Under. The 14-3 record was built on the most extreme one-score variance in NFL history, against the 26th-toughest offense schedule. The defense is real, but even a great defense can't sustain a .846 one-score record. Regression to 9-10 wins is the median expectation even if everything else stays the same.

## Prop angles

- **Bo Nix interceptions over**: More aggressive play-calling from Webb + Nix's pressure issues (took sacks at #1 rate on Payton's conservative scheme) = more turnovers when attacking downfield. INT props look attractive early in the season as the scheme adjusts.
- **J.K. Dobbins**: Rush attempts over if healthy — he's elite per-carry (5.0 YPC) behind an #1 OL; the concern is health, not opportunity.
- **Courtland Sutton**: Target share over early season — Waddle will earn his targets over time but Sutton is the established option; Sutton over on first-half-season yardage is reasonable.
- **Jaylen Waddle**: Attack late-season volume props when role is established; his per-route efficiency is elite; avoid early-season overreactions.
- **Nik Bonitto**: Sacks over — 14 sacks in 2025 from the #2 pressure rate spot; legitimate 15-18 sack ceiling in 2026.
- **Game totals**: Lean Under in Broncos road games against top-10 offenses — offense is still limited by Nix; defense may not be able to cover poor offenses the way it did in 2025.
