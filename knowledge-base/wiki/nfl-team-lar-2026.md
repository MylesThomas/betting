## Stafford's MVP 2025: the key storylines

**Stafford's 2025** was built on two specific McVay adjustments Sharp had lobbied for in his 2025 preview:

**1. Game scripting fixed**:
- 2024: #29 in first-half points/drive, #26 EPA/play, #27 score rate
- 2025: **#2 in first-half points/drive, #3 score rate, #1 lowest punt rate**

**2. Personnel shift** (away from 11-personnel, toward multi-TE):
- 2022-2024: 89-97% 11-personnel snaps (McVay's signature); red zone: 82-97% 11-personnel
- 2025: **59% 11-personnel** full field; **53% in red zone** — pivot to 2+ TE sets inside the 10
- 2+ TE red zone usage: 24.1% in 2025 (was 7.9% in 2024)
- Result: Stafford threw 20 TDs to 0 INTs on under-center play action; RZ EPA/play jumped to #1

The pre-snap motion split shows the Rams' ruthless efficiency:
- With PSM: 49% success rate, 8.1 YPA, +0.18 EPA
- Without PSM: 52% success rate, 8.0 YPA, +0.15 EPA
- *Both splits are elite* — this offense is efficiently orchestrated regardless of formation

**Stafford's 2025 pressure stats**: 31.8% pressure rate (#4 best); 3.7% sack rate (#2 best) — the OL protected him and he exploited the time.

## Why 2025 ended without a Super Bowl: special teams catastrophe

The Rams went **1-4 in games decided by a field goal** — the difference between hosting a bye and being a Wild Card team. Three specific examples:
1. **vs Philadelphia (Wk3)**: Kicker Joshua Karty blocked FG in 4th quarter, then 44-yard game-winner blocked as time expired — L
2. **vs San Francisco (Wk5)**: Tied in 4th, watched Karty's extra point get blocked to take lead, Rams lost in OT by 3
3. **at Seattle (Wk16)**: Up 30-14 with under 10 minutes; SEA returned a punt 58 yards for TD; Harrison Mevis missed his first career FG at 2:07; Rams scored TD on opening OT possession, SEA scored to win — **Rams became the first team in NFL history to lose an OT game after scoring a TD on the opening drive**

**Special teams coordinator Chase Blackburn was fired two days after Week 16.**

The Rams went Wild Card instead of the #1 seed and lost away games in Charlotte, Chicago, and Seattle (in that order).

The fix was direct: hired **Bubba Ventrone** (one of most respected STC in league) + signed **Joe Cardona** as long snapper stability.

## The Myles Garrett trade: the ceiling move

The price was steep:
- Gave: **Jared Verse** (2024 Defensive Rookie of Year) + 2027 1st + 2028 2nd + 2029 3rd
- Got: **Myles Garrett** (23 sacks in 2025, NFL record; 17.1% pressure rate; 6 straight seasons of 12+ sacks; Defensive Player of Year)

The Rams already had Byron Young (12.5 sacks) and Kobie Turner (7 sacks, 12.4% pressure rate). Adding Garrett takes a legitimate pass rush to a potentially historic one.

One context note from Sharp: "Last year, the Browns trailed on 61% of snaps, #7 most in the NFL. The Rams trailed on only 34% of snaps, #7 fewest. The difference is massive and will allow Garrett many more chances to get after opposing quarterbacks."

Front 7 jumped from #17 → #3 in unit rankings.

## The secondary rebuild

The biggest hole on the 2025 Rams was cornerback. Two moves fixed it:
1. **Trent McDuffie** (trade from KC): All-Pro; 4yr/$124M — highest-paid CB in NFL history
2. **Jaylen Watson** (FA from KC): 3yr/$51M — former McDuffie teammate; chemistry is established

Both played together as the top CB duo in Kansas City's championship secondary. The Rams also retained **Quentin Lake** and **Kam Curl** as the safety tandem.

Secondary jumped from #26 → #4 in unit rankings.

---

## McVay pivot: the 2+ TE identity

McVay pivoted from the #1 11-personnel rate in the NFL to a league-average 59% — a seismic shift for a coach who ran 89-97% for three straight years. Inside the red zone, 2+ TE sets jumped from 7.9% (2024) to 24.1% (2025).

The early-season proof (before Nacua's ankle sprained in Week 6):
- 2025 Weeks 1-5: 21% multi-TE snaps (highest in prior five years)
- When Nacua went down, McVay called **eight straight heavy-set run plays** — and went on a run
- "McVay tasted the forbidden heavy-personnel fruit, and he became a changed man"

The 2026 continuation: Klare (2nd round TE) gives more rotational depth to sustain this identity through depth.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- **The only team with all seven units ranked top 10** — no positional weakness
- Special teams is directly fixed (Ventrone + Cardona); the catastrophe that cost them the 1-seed and playoff home games is a known and corrected problem
- Myles Garrett + Byron Young = possibly the best EDGE tandem in NFL history in terms of accumulated pressure; the Stafford front is already protected (#4 pressure rate, #2 sack rate)
- Trent McDuffie + Jaylen Watson as the top CB duo addresses the only real hole from 2025
- Harrison Mevis was excellent all year except for one kick; the infrastructure around him is now fixed
- Super Bowl is in Los Angeles (SoFi Stadium) — this roster was **built around hosting this game**
- Sharp: "The Rams are the ultimate 'win-now' team"

**Under argument**
- Schedule jumps from #8 easiest (2025) to #5 toughest in 2026; worst from #8 to #5 is a 27-spot swing
- Opens season in **Australia** (international game Week 1) — surrenders a home game, jet lag disruption
- Net rest #24, Negated bye #29 — rest structure creates real disadvantage in close games
- Stafford is 38 years old in his 17th season; back history from training camp 2025 is documented
- Receivers **#4** but Davante Adams had 32 end-zone targets in 2025 — the most ever recorded; TD regression is expected
- Jared Verse is gone; Keagen Trost (3rd round) is the depth at tackle; OL depth is thinner
- Ty Simpson (#13 pick) provides no 2026 help and diverts capital that could have addressed immediate needs
- NFC West is not a cupcake division; 49ers, Seahawks, and Cardinals are all improved

**Sharp lean**: Slight Over / Neutral. The roster is the deepest in the NFL. The special teams fix is direct and structural. Garrett + McDuffie + Watson are legitimate game-changers. The schedule and rest are real headwinds, but the talent level is high enough to absorb them. The window is open, the Super Bowl is in LA, and Stafford is still playing elite football. The risk is 38-year-old QB durability and schedule compression.

## Prop angles

- **Puka Nacua**: Receptions over — he is the volume king of this offense (129 in 2025); McDaniel-style target distribution will flow through him; floor is 110+ catches
- **Davante Adams**: TDs under relative to 2025 pricing — 14 TDs and 32 end-zone targets are both records that mathematically regress; 8-10 TDs is a more sustainable pace
- **Matthew Stafford**: TD over on full-season basis — the system produces elite TD rates and Stafford has been above the 7% TD rate line in this scheme; fade if back concerns emerge in August
- **Myles Garrett**: Sacks over — trailing on only 34% of snaps (compared to 61% in Cleveland); more pass-rush snaps in favorable positions with a better supporting cast; the 23-sack pace was partly situation-driven, but 18-20 is realistic
- **Kyren Williams**: Carries over — the clear bell cow with a 59.3% touch share; RB1 in the best run game in football
- **Game totals**: Lean Over in LAR home games vs non-elite defenses early — the offense is #1 in efficiency with Stafford healthy and the McVay scheme at peak; road games in Weeks 9-15 are harder to project given rest disadvantage
