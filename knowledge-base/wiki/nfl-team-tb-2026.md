## The 2025 luck problem

Tampa's 2025 record was propped up by elite situational luck — nearly all of it regressive:

- **Turnover margin: +7 (#6)** — highly regressive
- **Fumble recovery FROE: +3.5 (#7)** — highly regressive
- **Penalty margin: -33 (#2 best)** — historically clean; unlikely to repeat
- **Net penalty EPA: +21.38 (#3)** — same regression concern
- **Player health**: Total #24, Offensive #29 (OL was #29 — all starters missed games), Defensive #8

The OL (#4 ranked unit) was among the sickest in football. If they return healthy, that's legitimate upside; if 2025 was just unusually good defensive health covering offensive injuries, the equilibrium is less clear.

## Baker Mayfield: the regression story

The 2024-to-2025 collapse was dramatic:

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 |
|--------|------|------|
| EPA/att | +0.30 (#10) | -0.01 |
| 4th quarter EPA/att | — | -0.10 (**#38 — worst**) |
| Man coverage EPA/att | — | -0.15 (#35) |

Bowles' defense allowed **69.8% red zone TD rate (#32 — worst in NFL)**, costing possessions the offense needed. The defense ranked **#18 EPA per play for 4 straight consecutive years** — consistently middle-of-the-pack regardless of personnel changes.

**The first-half problem**: Defense ranked #29 EPA and #26 success in first halves — digging holes Mayfield had to escape.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- OL (#4) is legitimately elite when healthy — Tristan Wirfs anchor; full health returns real upside
- Egbuka proved he can carry WR1 role as a rookie; 127 targets absorbed Evans' production
- Robinson's McVay-tree system should suit Mayfield better than a revolving door of coordinators
- First 4 home games in Florida heat give TB a structural early-season edge
- NFC South is weak — Tampa is the favorite to win the division at 8.5 wins
- Defensive health should be stable; Bain adds an immediate pass-rush upgrade

**Under argument**
- **Bowles defense: #18 EPA per play for 4 consecutive years** — scheme-ceiling problem that doesn't change with personnel
- **Baker Mayfield's 4th quarter EPA was #38 (worst in NFL)** — cannot close games
- Red zone defense: 69.8% TD rate allowed (#32 — worst); they let opponents score
- Turnover margin +7, penalty luck +21 EPA — both regress hard; losing either costs 1-2 wins
- OL injury history (#29 offensive health): all starters missed games; same injury risk in 2026
- Mike Evans is gone; Godwin missed most of 2025; the WR corps relies entirely on an unproven rookie

**Sharp lean (Grade A — NFC South ceiling team)**: "Todd Bowles is on the hot seat, and Baker Mayfield enters a contract year... Tampa can still hang around the NFC South because the division is weak." Slight Under. The luck components from 2025 are nearly all regressive. Bowles cannot scheme his way past a structural defensive ceiling, and Mayfield's fourth-quarter collapse was the worst in the NFL. Winning the weak NFC South at 8-9 wins is realistic; competing beyond that is not.

## Prop angles

- **Emeka Egbuka**: Targets and receptions over — clear WR1 with 127 rookie targets; Robinson's system will lean on the best weapon
- **Baker Mayfield**: Passing yards over (volume is guaranteed); fade 4th-quarter comeback props — he ranked #38 worst in clutch
- **Chris Godwin**: Game-by-game targets over when healthy — slot role with Godwin healthy is productive; full-season props require health caveat
- **Bucky Irving**: Rush yards — OL is elite when healthy; Irving's speed/receiving package is underutilized; upside if OL stays healthy
- **Game totals**: Lean Over at home early-season when Florida heat disrupts road offenses; Lean Under TB road games where Mayfield's 4th-quarter collapse is exposed in close games
