# Washington Commanders 2026

**Sharp forecast**: 7.5 wins — **Division Rank #3 (NFC East)**

---

## Coaching & scheme

- HC: **Dan Quinn** (Year 3; HC rank #22 — fell from #13, -9 spots; fired DC mid-season 2025)
- OC: **David Blough** (new; Ben Johnson/Dan Campbell tree from Detroit/Chicago; wants Daniels under center, play action, motion — complete antithesis of Kingsbury's shotgun-heavy system)
- DC: **Daronte Jones** (new; 3 seasons with Brian Flores in Minnesota; Flores-scheme approach with heavy blitz and disguise; aligns with Sonny Styles pick)

**Kliff Kingsbury fired**: In 2 years, Daniels took 33 career dropbacks from under center (total). Used pre-snap motion at 45.6% (#24) and play action 24.8% (#18). Ran no-huddle on 1,279 plays — 815 more than any other team. Blough explicitly reverses all of this.

## The Daniels injury catastrophe

Daniels appeared on the injury report in 16 of 18 weeks in 2025:
- Week 2: Wrist; Weeks 3-4: Knee (OUT); Week 5: Knee; Weeks 6-7: Knee; Week 8: Hamstring (OUT); Week 9: Hamstring
- **Week 9 (SEA, trailing 38-7, 4th quarter)**: Sacked by Drake Thomas/Nwosu/Jarran Reed, planted non-throwing left hand, **dislocated left elbow** — visible, grotesque injury; taken off in air cast
- Weeks 10-11: Elbow (OUT); Week 12: Bye; Weeks 13: Elbow (OUT)
- Week 14: Quinn unwisely brought back a compromised Daniels vs MIN → 9/20, 78 yds, INT, re-dislocated — a decision Sharp calls "borderline criminal"
- Weeks 15-18: Elbow (OUT)

**Daniels was healthy for exactly ONE game in 2025**. When healthy, he still flashed elite traits — **deep ball was #2 in NFL (0.92 EPA/att)** and **2-High coverage was #3 (0.23 EPA/att)**.

**Health context**: Total player health #30, Offensive #30, Defensive #27. Nine starters missed at least 7 games. This was the most injured roster in recent Washington history.

## The 2025 defense: historically bad

Washington's defense was, by most measures, the worst in the NFL — and in some categories, the worst this century:

- **#32 down set conversion rate; #31 EPA per play; #31 early down success rate**
- Allowed 384 yards per game — **most of any team in the NFL**
- First quarter: allowed 101 total points (#28), forced punts on only **17.9% of opponent opening drives (#1 worst for any defense this century)**
- 4 consecutive blowout losses by 21+ points — first since 2002 Arizona Cardinals
- Red zone: 37% of opponent drives reached it (#28); when they did, allowed 68% TDs (#31)
- Only the second defense in the last 5 years to allow over 35% of drives reaching RZ AND 67%+ TD rate

**Dan Quinn's defense in Dallas (2021-2023)**: #1 takeaways, #1 turnover margin, #1 points off turnovers. Washington 2025: #29-#30 in all of the above. His scheme works when it generates turnovers. It doesn't when it can't.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- **Daniels health regression**: was #30 total health in 2025; the elbow/knee/hamstring sequence was compounding and freak; a healthy Daniels in 2026 unlocks a legitimate playoff team
- When healthy, deep ball #2 and 2-High #3 are elite marks — the arm talent is real; Blough's under-center scheme removes the Kingsbury constraints
- McLaurin + Okonkwo + Williams + White form a real weapon set; the receiving corps was effectively absent in 2025
- Defense is genuinely rebuilt: Oweh + Chaisson + Omenihu on the edge; Sonny Styles anchors the LB; Daronte Jones' scheme is proven
- **Draft capital: #1 in NFL** by over-expected value — Styles (2% missed tackles) is a steal at #7; the front office is executing
- Net rest #4; zero SNF/MNF off road; bye intact — schedule structure is favorable
- Schedule #13 moderate; not the toughest slate

**Under argument**
- **RBs: #32 (dead last)** and **Receivers: #31** — Blough's offensive system needs weapons; they don't have enough
- OL: #22; Conerly Jr. allowed 8 sacks/43 pressures — if Daniels gets hurt behind this line again, the season is over
- Quinn's defense was historically bad in 2025 despite spending DL #1 ($106.86M cap); Daronte Jones must rebuild an entirely new unit from scratch
- Secondary: #31 — Trey Amos (74th in coverage), a thin CB room, and questions at safety
- Turnover margin: -13 (#31) in 2025; Quinn's unit couldn't generate havoc; if DC Jones doesn't deliver takeaways, the defense collapses
- HC rank fell #13 → #22 (-9 spots) — panel losing confidence in Quinn's decision-making (keeping Daniels in the SEA game is Exhibit A)
- 1-score record: 2-5 (.286, #26); FG games: 0-3 (.000) — cannot win close games structurally

**Sharp lean (Grade C)**: "Washington's fortunes revolve around the health of Jayden Daniels. The positive is that before injuries derailed his 2025 season, he still flashed franchise-quarterback traits, and the team was competitive. If Daniels stays healthy, this team can compete for a playoff spot. If not, their ceiling lowers dramatically, and the 2024 season begins to look more like an outlier." Slight Over (health-gated). The talent is real — Daniels, McLaurin, a rebuilt defensive front — but nearly every unit ranks in the bottom third. The 2026 season is entirely binary on whether Washington stops putting Jayden Daniels in danger.

## Prop angles

- **Terry McLaurin**: Targets and yards over — Blough's explicit goal is 10+ targets per game for McLaurin; under-center motion-based offense unlocks his route tree; his target share collapses when Daniels is out
- **Jayden Daniels**: Rushing yards over (health-gated) — his 2024 rushing showed elite elusiveness; Blough's system will use designed QB runs; but any injury changes everything
- **Jayden Daniels**: Deep ball props over — 0.92 EPA/att (#2 in NFL) on deep throws is elite and wasn't a 2025 sample; when healthy and vertical, he is one of the best deep passers in the NFL
- **Chig Okonkwo**: Targets over — slot TE in under-center, motion offense is the primary 3rd-down option; team re-signed him for exactly this role
- **Odafe Oweh**: Sacks over — 15.9% pressure rate with a scheme (Flores/Jones) built to generate pressure and takeaways; his $100M contract comes with usage
- **Game totals**: Fade Washington totals in any game where Daniels is on the injury report; back Washington totals hard when he's fully healthy vs weak pass defenses
