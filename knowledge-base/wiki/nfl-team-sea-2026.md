## How they actually won the Super Bowl

Before 2025, Seattle was predicted at 7.5 wins. They went 14-3 with Darnold leading the league in turnovers. The explanation is elite coaching around severe personnel limitations.

**Klint Kubiak's four key offensive changes:**
1. **Personnel**: Shifted from 11-heavy (78% 3WR in 2024, ranked #30 EPA) → heavy multi-TE (43% 2TE in 2025, #7); 2-TE results: #2 EPA, #1 yards/play, #3 success
2. **Play action**: From 19% usage in 2024 (rare; results: -0.15 EPA, #31) → 28% usage in 2025; results: +0.28 EPA (#6), 52% success (#8), 11.3 YPA (#1)
3. **Pre-snap motion**: From 42% in 2024 (terrible: -0.31 EPA per play, #31) → 54% in 2025; results: 8.2 yds/pass (#2)
4. **First down**: Was #23 EPA in 2024 → #7 EPA in 2025

**Jaxson Smith-Njigba's emergence**: His 36.2% target share was the highest in the NFL. He led the league in deep receiving yards. Kubiak turned a slot receiver into one of the best WRs in football.

**Macdonald's defense**: From Week 7-14, allowed -0.34 EPA/play — the best 8-game stretch by any defense in 25 years (better than the 2013 Legion of Boom, 2002 Bucs, 2003 Ravens). Finished #1 in EPA/play (-0.12), #2 yards/play (4.6), #3 success rate (37%). Did this with the **#11 lowest blitz rate** while generating the **#6 best pressure rate (39.9%)** — a remarkable achievement through simulated pressure disguise.

**The run defense**: #1 in yards per carry allowed (3.5), #1 in EPA per rush (-0.22), #2 in success rate (31%). -0.22 EPA per rush was the lowest for any defense over the last 5 years.

## The 2026 question: can Fleury preserve what Kubiak built?

This is the central concern. Kubiak's system was the engine. The specific scheme changes — multi-TE sets, play action at 28%, motion-driven passing — elevated Sam Darnold from a career bridge QB to a Super Bowl winner.

Brian Fleury has a TE background and ran similar concepts at San Francisco. But:
- This is Darnold's **latest in a series of new coordinators** in essentially every season of his career
- The continuity that unlocked him in Minnesota (one year) and Seattle (one year) is gone the moment he finally won a ring
- Fleury will need to rebuild the system trust from scratch

**The turnover problem doesn't disappear**: Darnold threw 14 INTs and lost 13 fumbles — 28 total turnovers, most of any individual player in the NFL. The defense buried those mistakes. Without a historically elite defense AND the same scheme, the turnovers become far more costly.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Defense is legitimately **the best unit in football** — #2 secondary, #5 front 7; Macdonald's scheme is intact; Witherspoon + Emmanwori + Jones all return
- OL is fully intact (all 5 starters + 6 reserves returning) and improved 21 spots in 2025; Darnold will stay upright
- Smith-Njigba locked in at $168.6M — the most dominant receiver in the league; offenses need their WR1 to work
- Fleury's TE background fits the multi-TE scheme that made Seattle's offense click; the structural innovation can survive the OC change
- Net rest #4 — excellent structural advantage; no games off road SNF/MNF; bye intact
- Macdonald has won 24 games and a Super Bowl in 2 seasons — the coaching edge is real and repeatable

**Under argument**
- **Klint Kubiak's departure is the single biggest risk** — the scheme architect is gone; Darnold has had a new play-caller every year of his career; continuity gone
- **RBs are #31** — Kenneth Walker (161 SB scrimmage yards) is in Kansas City; Charbonnet out most of 2026 with ACL; Price has ball security issues (4 fumbles on 295 touches)
- Darnold's 28 total turnovers in 2025 were the most of any player in the NFL — the defense absorbed them; if the defense regresses from historically elite to merely very good, those turnovers produce losses
- **Inside red zone: -0.15 EPA/att (#37 — worst)** — Darnold cannot score when it matters most; the defense scoring 7 TDs itself (#1 non-offensive TDs) masked this
- Cooper Kupp at 33 had career-low production; the WR2 and WR3 spots are thin
- Schedule is genuinely harder; opponents will have Macdonald's defense on film for 2 full seasons

**Sharp lean (Grade A++)**: "Seattle's foundation looks sustainable, built with young talent across all key positional groups, and there is no doubt they will be a contender this season and beyond." Slight Over. The defense gives Seattle the right to believe, and Macdonald is a legitimate generational coaching talent. But the OC change combined with the worst RB room in the NFL and Darnold's red zone/turnover issues creates meaningful downside risk.

## Prop angles

- **Jaxson Smith-Njigba**: Receptions and yards over — he is the entire offense; the system may change but his target share is irreplaceable; 100+ catches is the floor in a healthy Darnold season
- **Sam Darnold**: Passing yards over — the system wants volume; 4,000+ yards is likely with a healthy OL; but fade passing TD props given red zone weakness
- **AJ Barner**: Targets over — his 68-target season should grow with Walker gone (fewer rushing plays, more passing downs); TE role in multi-TE sets is critical
- **Jadarian Price**: Fade rushing TDs — ball security and goal-line concerns are real; Wilson is the goal-line back
- **Game totals**: Lean Under when defense is full-strength vs average QBs; the defense alone can carry games below average totals; Lean Over road games where Darnold's turnover issues are exposed
