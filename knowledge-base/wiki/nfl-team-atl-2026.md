## The big problem: second-half collapses

Falcons led at halftime in 9 games, won 7 (good). But trailed at halftime → went 0-4. Third quarter scoring: **2.88 pts/game (28th), -54 margin (28th)** — outscored by 54 points in Q3 alone.

Bijan Robinson first-half YPC: **6.3** → second-half: **4.1**. Same personnel, same down usage, same concepts. The run game just broke at halftime. Without the run game, 3rd-down distances ballooned (6.4 yds to go in 1H → 8.6 in 2H), drives shortened, scoring dropped.

Stefanski's system is specifically designed to control second halves — this is the core investment thesis for Atlanta in 2026.

## Betting angles

**Over argument**
- Bijan Robinson in Stefanski's wide-zone system with Bill Callahan coaching OL = cleanest path to offensive identity this franchise has ever had
- Negative fumble luck in 2025: Falcons lost 10 of 19 fumbles, opponents lost only 7 of 23 → expect regression
- Drake London missed 4 games; healthier season = more production (tied for most receptions among WRs in weeks he played)
- Allgeier gone → Robinson gets more goal-line reps
- Average record in one-score games; variance will normalize

**Under argument**
- QB situation is genuinely broken: Penix ACL recovery timeline uncertain, Tua has documented concussion history and has regressed
- Front 7 is #31; 2025 pass rush dismantled: Floyd, Ebiketie, Onyemata all gone; James Pearce Jr. (10.5 sacks in 2025) faces legal situation (arrested for aggravated battery — pretrial diversion; availability in Week 7 uncertain)
- Secondary dropped to #22 without enough top-end reinforcement
- Stefanski went 8-26 in his last two seasons in Cleveland
- 2-5 vs teams with winning records in 2025

**Sharp lean**: Under. The ceiling is real but the QB situation is too uncertain and the front 7 is genuinely depleted. 7.5 is a fair number; fade unless QB1 is confirmed healthy.

## Prop angles

- **Bijan Robinson**: Extreme over value on rush attempts in a Stefanski-led offense. Target 25+ carries per game props. Wide-zone also generates receiving work (103 targets in 2025). Elite floor.
- **Drake London**: Target share props — when healthy he runs 30% of routes and gets 28-32% of targets. Fade early-season yardage props if ACL recovery uncertain; attack mid-season props once cleared.
- **Kyle Pitts**: Consistent target floor (118 in 2025); best as a receiver, not just a blocker. TE1-level props in favorable matchups.
- **James Pearce Jr.**: Sack props — high uncertainty. Do not bet until legal/availability situation resolves in Week 6-7.
